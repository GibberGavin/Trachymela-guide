Cuticular Wax Patterns

Many species of Trachymela are covered in a layer of cuticular wax that forms distinct patterns. However, this wax is often fragile; in specimens preserved in alcohol, it is usually entirely missing, and in older dead specimens, it may be fully or partially absent. Recognizing these six primary patterns can be essential for identifying fresh or well-preserved specimens.
