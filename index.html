A Guide to the genus Trachymela

An interactive resource to aid in the identification of the species of this large and taxonomically difficult genus of leaf beetles. The guide makes use of the Lucid identification system as the key, allowing character states to be specified in any order.

INTRODUCTION USING THE KEY IDENTIFY (LUCID) SPECIES INDEX TAXONOMY GLOSSARY

The leaf beetle genus Trachymela (Chrysomelidae : Chrysomelinae) consists of a large number of species, characterized by their warty dorsal surface, often cryptic brown to black coloration with a dorsal surface covered in a layer of granular wax. This guide aims to assist in resolving the difficulties in identifying these closely related species by utilizing a combination of morphological features and measurements, geographic distribution and host plant associations.

Whether you are working with live field specimens or museum collections, start with the Introduction for an overview of the genus or use the Identify button to begin a Lucid identification session. The Using the Key button takes you to a detailed discussion of the morphological features of the insects and how they are used in the key.

A cautionary note: This guide is a work in progress. Although the key includes all of the known species, some are not fully documented as yet, with some species description, especially for species in the west and north of the continent, not yet completed and not fully coded in the key. Much of the explanatory material (in the "Using the Key", "Taxonomy" and "Glossary" sections) still needs to be added.
