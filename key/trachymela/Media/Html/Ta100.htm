Aedeagus, lateral view (Gibraltar Range NP, NE NSW, 25-055389)

Distribution

Ta100

Name

Trachymela sp. Ta100

Reference specimen

25-055389, Gibraltar Range N.P., N.E. New South Wales

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 9.1 mm (1), ♂ 8.6 mm (1).

[description based on one male and one female specimen]

Colour:  head dark-brown, sometimes with some black in area anterior to fronto-clypeal suture, labrum light-brown, mandibles reddish-brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-2 light to mid-brown, 5-11 much darker, often black, with 3-4 transitioning between these; pronotum: brown, same or fractionally darker shade than head, grading smoothly to a slightly lighter shade in lateral marginal region, a near-circular black patch on either side of centre and a smaller circular black patch behind each eye, anterior and lateral edges dark-brown; scutellum: black;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with prominent black raised, shiny, smooth pustules studding the whole surface, a concentration of these is situated immediately behind antero-median depression with black raised interstices and smaller verrucae sometimes partially or completely connecting them transversely, some longitudinally extended and prominent pustules are present in posterior half of elytra, elytral suture black at least in posterior half, anterior margin narrowly edged in black, lateral edges black, punctures are similar in colour as interstices, humeral callus black; venter: head, thoracic and abdominal ventrites and legs black, epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: unknown.

Shape: slightly flattened in front of greatest height, greatest height viewed laterally is at around middle of elytral margin; Head; punctures relatively small (pd~1.5xef), evenly and densely packed. Antennae long, especially in male (AL ♂=54.7%BL, ♀=34.6%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression barely perceptible with a clearly defined and almost round elevated and finely and sparsely punctured area in middle, puncturation clearly impressed, relatively fine (similar to that on head), evenly and moderately densely distributed in discal area, becoming coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins, interstices smooth and shiny in discal area.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; smooth and unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely, lightly curved longitudinally in anterior half, then more strongly curved, antero-median depression distinct but shallow depression, punctures large and distinct and relatively evenly distributed between the large and prominent verrucae, verrucae are mostly unpunctured or with a few scattered very fine punctures, many are longitudinally extended, some considerably longer than wide, and placed in interrupted series, verrucae tend to crowd together more just posterior to antero-median depression where they may be loosely linked transversely via dark, elevated interstices, suture slightly carinate in posterior quarter, posterior half of marginal sulcus well-defined, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleurae slight (HCE/PW=0.31-0.33).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided or slightly narrower anteriorly, closed anteriorly, short setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; very short and broad (LPE=18%BL), in dorsal view, sides broadening by diverging from base almost linearly with only a small inflection just anterior to base with widest point (WPE=0.53*LPE) close to apex, sides connect to anterior edge in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with anterior edge very slightly sinuous, almost straight and with a small triangular tip protruding from its centre, dorso-lateral channels well-defined but short, ostial flaps short, dorso-median channel short, ostium quite small, with a large, flat and semi-transparent area on either side; in lateral view, fairly evenly and moderately curved over most of its length until about 15% of distance from apex, where there is a sharp downward bend, tip very slightly deflexed, flagellum moderately thin at exit.

Similar species

Several species have prominent black verrucae and feature the characteristic mixture of brown and grey dust as seen in Ta100.  See discussion under Ta22.

Notes

The male of this species has exceptionally long antennae as well as an aedeagus with a rather unusual shape, vaguely resembling that of Ta46.  The species has been collected from an unidentified smooth-barked eucalypt.
