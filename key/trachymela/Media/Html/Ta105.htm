Ta105

Name

Trachymela seriata (Germar)

Reference specimen

2004-538, Halls Gap, western Victoria.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 6.9-7.6 mm [8], ♂ 5.8-7.3 mm [15].

Colour:  head: mid- to dark-brown with clypeus usually at least partially black, sometimes completely black, rarely with no black colouration, labrum straw-brown, sometimes infused with black patches, mandibles dark-brown with black tips or completely black; antennomeres 1-3 uniformly straw-brown to light-brown, 4-11 considerably darker brown or, less often, black; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, a small, usually roughly oval-shaped dark-brown or black mark present at edge of disc, behind inside level of eye, rarely is this mark more extended towards posterior margin, very rarely it extends obliquely towards centre and posteriorly; a smaller, dark-brown or black round, elevated pimple usually present in centre of foveal depression, rarely pronotum is completely devoid of dark markings;  scutellum: brown, same or a little darker shade than adjacent region of pronotum, usually broadly edged in darker-brown; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, punctures are same shade as interstices, surface is scattered with small to moderate sized (3-6 pd) clearly elevated smooth black verrucae, sometimes arranged in an almost random fashion over surface of disc, but more often with many arranged in 4-5 longitudinal series, with other black verrucae scattered between these series, rarely are adjacent verrucae within series joined into elevated black ridges, a row of prominent black verrucae is almost invariably present along discal margin, some black sometimes present on elytral summit, humeral callus black; venter: uniformly dark reddish-brown, including legs, abdominal ventrites sometimes slightly paler, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown, very rarely black.  Cuticular wax: sparse to quite thick, relatively evenly to slightly unevenly distributed layer of light-brown wax.

Shape: slightly flattened (height=0.38-0.44%BL) almost evenly curved from scutellum to at least a little over half of distance to apex where curvature usually increases noticeably in males, greatest height viewed laterally a little to well in front of middle.  Head; puncture size moderate (pd~1.5-2.5xef), clearly impressed and closely but slightly unevenly distributed, adjacent punctures sometimes almost touching. Antennae short (AL ♂=36-40%BL, ♀=34-37%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression absent or very shallow, a prominently elevated round pustule situated behind centre of eye midway between anterior and posterior edges, discal area with surface relatively smooth, often shiny, punctures clearly impressed and similar to or fractionally smaller but more evenly-sized than those on head, evenly to slightly unevenly and moderately densely to sparsely distributed (spacing~2.5-4pd), becoming much coarser towards lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving almost imperceptibly and smoothly towards rear margin with the curvature becoming greater in posterior third, broadest about 2/3 of distance to posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; smooth or rough textured, unpunctured or sparsely punctured with very shallow punctures.  Elytra; surface evenly curved laterally, punctures moderately sized, closely spaced and quite evenly distributed over all of disc, prominent verrucae, mostly from 2-4 pd in diameter and predominately smooth and unpunctured, are dotted over surface of disc, sometimes almost randomly distributed, but more often with many in 4 or 5 longitudinal series on disc as well as a line of them along discal margin, occasionally adjacent verrucae merged to form longitudinal ridges, elytral suture not carinate in posterior third, surface continuous (rarely slightly discontinuous) under humeral callus. Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.34-0.35).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel or narrowing anteriorly, lightly closed anteriorly, prosternal process and mesosternum devoid of long setae, a single long seta present on anterior, median and posterior trochanters; front edge of metasternum beveled, flat; inner edge of posterior of epipleurae lacking setae.  Aedeagus; moderate length (LPE=28-33%BL), lanceolate, in dorsal view, widest at or just anterior to basal sulcus with sides converging in straight line or slightly sinuous arc that is very slightly but noticeably concave a little before the apex making for a quite sharp tip, dorsal surface membranous with a very faint dorso-median channel present, ostium very small, tip triangular; in lateral view, sharply bent at 120˚ at about 40% of length from basal extreme, lower edge towards apex from bend quite straight until close to apex where it bends downwards very slightly, dorsal surface elevated into a blunt peak at about 20% posterior from apex, tip deflexed.

Similar species

Ta03 – in most aspects, Ta03 is very similar to Ta105.  The two species have an almost identically shaped and sized aedeagus.   The elytral verrucae on Ta105 are usually more discrete, even if expanded longitudinally slightly, compared to the relatively long ridges present on the elytral disc of Ta03, though occasional exceptions to this have been found. 

Ta29 – only the more southerly populations of this species resemble Ta105 quite closely.  However, there is a slight difference in the shape of the aedeagus, with that of Ta105 slightly more sharply pointed at the apex.

Notes

Identification of Ta105 as Trachymela seriata (Germar) is from the description and type locality only and is somewhat provisional.

This is a member of the triangulum group of species, distinguished by their lanceolate aedeagus.  Verrucae are usually discrete and mostly arranged in linear series, but a few individuals have long ridges along parts of these series.

Ta105 has been collected from Eucalyptus aromaphloia (Symphyomyrtus : Maidenaria) and E. baxteri (Eucalyptus : Capillulus).
