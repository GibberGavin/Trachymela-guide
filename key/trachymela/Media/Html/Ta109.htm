Name

Trachymela sp. Ta109

Reference specimen

2005-185, Kroombit Tops, Central Queensland.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 7.82 mm (1), ♂ 7.25 mm (1).

Colour:  head: uniformly mid-brown, labrum straw-brown, mandibles with black tips; antennomeres 1-4 uniformly straw-brown, 5-11 same shade or grading fractionally darker towards apex, the darker shade may be confined to distal region of antennomeres; pronotum: brown, same or fractionally darker shade as head; scutellum: brown, similar shade to pronotum, fractionally darker along margins;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, suture bordered in black from scutellum to elytral summit, with this relatively thin border expanding into a roughly round black blotch at summit; small to moderate-sized, round, raised black tubercles scattered over disc but not in antero-median depression, a cluster of these are situated between humeral callus and scutellum with a longitudinal, sometimes interrupted black ridge in middle, two transversely oriented bands of black verrucae are present behind the antero-median depression: one, directly posterior to depression, extends from just below summit to about midpoint of discal margin, the other runs from just below summit and a little behind the first to discal margin at about 75% of its length, verrucae in 1st band may be closely spaced in parts to form an interrupted transverse ridge; a scatter of dark-brown or black verrucae are present posterior to 2nd band, some of these may roughly align longitudinally; humeral callus black or darker brown than surrounds; venter: mostly, including legs, medium brown with all or parts of thoracic ventrites much darker brown or even black, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: light-brown, distribution unknown.

Shape: almost evenly rounded from scutellum to apex, females with curvature increasing noticeably just before apex, greatest height viewed laterally clearly in front of middle of elytral margin (PGH ♂=51%BL, ♀=53%BL), ; Head; punctures quite large (pd~2-3xef), mostly evenly and densely packed.  Antennae moderate in length (AL ♂=43.3%BL, ♀=36.8%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, lateral margins not explanate, foveal depression absent, with a roughly round elevation directly behind eyes with puncturation continuous over it, discal area slightly uneven with puncturation shallow and poorly defined, of size similar to or slightly larger than on head, mostly evenly and moderately densely distributed in discal area, punctures become much coarser but slightly better defined in marginal region.  Thickened front margins smoothly join slightly thinner lateral margins in a rounded right angle, with lateral margin briefly straight before curving evenly towards posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; relatively smooth, or with a scatter of very shallow punctures.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over discal region, not or only very slightly out-turned at margin,  a shallow depression extends transversely across surface just in front of middle, punctures moderately large, clearly defined and relatively evenly distributed in anterior two-thirds of disc (PC~32/mm2), set deeply among the network of interstices sometimes with a very light scatter of fine punctures on interstices, moderate-sized (dark) verrucae, sometimes with a small puncture in centre, are scattered over much of elytral disc, with the distribution described in the colour section above, but are absent from anterio-median depression, punctures somewhat larger and more widely spaced in marginal area, suture carinate in posterior quarter, surface continuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.34-0.35).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided over much of its length, lightly closed anteriorly, short setae very sparsely distributed over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=34.5%BL), in dorsal view, approximately parallel-sided and broad from basal sulcus to almost rear of ostium, where sides diverge slightly and linearly to the widest point, from where sides converge towards apex in a smooth and relatively tight arc, ostium almost quadrate, almost as wide as long, with well-defined dorso-median channel that continues a long way past posterior of ostial depression, dorsolateral channels well defined, two long ostial flaps protrude towards apex in the oval ostial depression, at level of tip; tip small and smoothly triangular; in lateral view, bent near basal sulcus, then lower surface almost straight until close to apex where it curves downward slightly, upper surface very slightly curved from basal sulcus, producing a gradual thinning towards apex, tip deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta43 – this species occurs in the same geographic area, is similar in the pattern of distribution of elytral verrucae and also has a common black area at the elytral summit.  It usually has a black mark in the foveal area of the pronotum and the elytral verrucae are more concentrated than those of Ta109, with a prominent, black transverse ridge near the middle of the discal margin.

Notes

Ta109 has been collected from an unspecified “smooth-barked” Eucalyptus.
