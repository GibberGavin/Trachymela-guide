Trachymela sp. Ta128

Reference specimen

2020-055a, Hughenden, Queensland.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 8.5-8.7 mm (2), ♂ 7.6-8.1 mm (3).

Colour:  head: frons and clypeus uniformly mid-brown, a strip bordering labrum sometimes fractionally darker, labrum yellow-brown, mandibles brown with black tips; antennae completely and uniformly pale brown; pronotum: brown, disc fractionally darker than head, with marginal areas slightly paler, a small, black round or oval spot present on each side centred just behind inside edge of eye and slightly in front of centre; scutellum: brown, same shade as pronotum, sometimes with slightly darker lateral edges; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with marginal area slightly paler in shade, elytral disc usually with several longitudinal rows (most prominently, VR3, VR4 and VR5) of black or dark-brown tubercles, many in the form of short weals, these verrucae are asynchronous between rows over the anterior ¾ of disc, but line up in two transverse rows in the apical ¼, punctures of same shade as interstices, humeral callus black; venter: almost completely orange-brown, sometimes with parts of ventrites, especially margins, slightly darker, legs similar shade with at most some fractionally darker patches on femurs, epipleura light-brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: uniformly light-brown and somewhat unevenly distributed.

Shape: almost uniformly and lightly curved for anterior 75% of length, where curvature increases noticeably, moderately high, elytral summit a little in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; puncture size variable (pd~1.5-2.5xef) with mixed sizes present along with much smaller punctures, quite evenly and densely distributed.  Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=35-37%BL, ♀=35.2%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely over discal area, with foveal depression absent and whole of foveal area almost imperceptibly elevated, puncturation on disc clearly impressed, similar in size as on head, quite evenly distributed and dense in discal area, interstices in discal area smooth, never obscuring punctures, punctures becoming much coarser towards margin.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin usually straight for almost half distance before curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; surface smooth, lightly punctured, punctures similar in size or somewhat smaller than those on adjacent surface of pronotum but much less clearly impressed.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely throughout with at most fractionally explanate margins, punctures moderately large and relatively evenly distributed over disc, noticeably larger in marginal area, 3 or 4 longitudinal series of verrucae, round or often considerably extended laterally, rarely with a very fine central puncture, are present on disc, these being VR3, VR4, VR5 and sometimes, VR7, verrucae from other rows may be present, but these are more often small and inconspicuous, see “colour” above for distribution of verrucae, elytral suture carinate in posterior third, lateral margin almost straight or very slightly ascending in anterior quarter, surface slightly discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.36-0.37).  Venter; Prosternal process quite wide, almost parallel-sided in anterior half, open anteriorly, a sparse scatter of short setae on prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderate-sized (LPE=35-37%BL), in dorsal view, sides converging fractionally in a smooth arc from basal sulcus for about half of length before diverging slightly to widest point near anterior end of ostium, from where the sides converge towards apex, initially in smooth and relatively tight arcs and then in straight lines, forming a triangular apex that lacks a projecting tip, spatula large, dorsal surface with well-defined dorso-median channel and dorsolateral channels resulting in two long ostial flaps, these are fused and more strongly sclerotized at their ends, forming a flat, rectangular process that is asymmetrical in being directed towards the left (viewed dorsally from behind); in lateral view, ventral surface almost evenly and lightly curved throughout, with apical half of spatula deflexed, dorsal surface converges very gradually and evenly with ventral surface from basal sulcus to near apical end of ostium, from where the convergence becomes more rapid, flagellum thin and curved.

Similar species

 Ta147 - this species may co-occur with Ta128 and is morphologically almost indistinguishable from it.  They have been found on different hosts (Ta128 has been collected from the desert bloodwood, C. terminalis).  The aedeagus is the only reliable way to distinguish these two species.

Notes

The antennae of the female are only slightly shorter than those of the male in this species.

Ta128 has been collected from the ghost gum species Corymbia dallachiana and C. gilbertensis (both section Blakearia) near Hughenden, northern Queensland, with a single record also from the red bloodwood, C. erythrophloia.  No host information is available for the Northern Territory specimen.  The location records for this species are rather widely separated, but there is little difference in the external morphology between them, with the exception of the aedeagus.  That of the northern Queensland male is substantially wider and the process at the end of the fused ostial flaps is much more oblique.  These may indicate specific distinction, but more material is needed to confirm this.
