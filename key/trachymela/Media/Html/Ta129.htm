Name

Trachymela sp. Ta129

Reference specimen

2007-086, Boulia, western Queensland.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 6.7-7.5 mm (4), ♂ 6.3-7.0 mm (4).

Colour:  head light- to mid-brown, almost invariably with a pair of large black patches behind fronto-clypeal suture, one on each side between eye and central suture, often merged across central suture into a single large patch, rarely much smaller or completely absent, labrum yellow-brown, mandibles brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-5 straw-brown, 7-11 similar or, rarely, grading fractionally darker; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with black markings in the form a black line of uneven thickness on each side of discal area extending from anterior to posterior edge, sometimes anterior edge of disc between these lines is black, often the foveal area has a small, round black or dark-brown spot in centre, rarely a thin dark-brown or black line extends along centre of disc from anterior to posterior edge; scutellum: brown, same shade as adjacent area of pronotum, with lateral edges lined in a darker shade;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with black markings as follows: a black ridge, either complete or composed of closely spaced elevated verrucae, extends posteriorly from base centred between scutellum and humeral callus and in line with the black line on pronotum, sometimes this ridge extends as a series of black verrucae almost to elytral apex, with an interruption always at a depression just in front of summit, the area between it and humeral callus near its anterior end is often substantially black, other small black or dark-brown verrucae are scattered over disc, sometimes aligned in longitudinal series and these may include black interstices connecting many of the verrucae, lateral margin of disc with a relatively large black patch near its middle and often with black or dark-brown elevated verrucae along it, a small common black area is situated across suture at its summit, punctures similar or fractionally darker than interstices, humeral callus variable, from little darker than surrounds to black; venter: light- to mid-brown with thoracic ventrites sometimes slightly darker, especially along margins and laterally, legs similar or slightly paler, sometimes with darker patches on femurs, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: a mostly uniformly distributed and moderately thick layer of brown, with faint greyish longitudinal bands present on the elytra along some of the rows of verrucae, most prominently along VR2, VR4 and along VR8/VR9 behind and just below level of humeral callus.  Greyish wax may be present on pronotum but is very sparse and tends to be more uniformly scattered among the brown.

Shape: narrow and relatively thin, almost evenly rounded from scutellum to apex, sometimes curvature increases considerably slightly just before apex, height=40-42%BL, greatest height viewed laterally is in front of middle of elytral margin.  Head; punctures small (pd~1.5-2.5xef) and relatively evenly and densely packed, with a scatter of much smaller punctures.  Antennae short (AL ♂=36-39%BL, ♀=30-34%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, becoming slightly explanate very close to lateral margin, foveal area depressed slightly just behind anterior edge, a round and more sparsely punctured pustule present in middle of foveal area, discal area slightly rugulose, its lateral boundary in the form of a slightly raised (black) ridge, a thin and sometimes interrupted central longitudinal ridge extends from anterior to posterior margins and surface between lateral and central ridges always noticeably elevated in an oblique central ridge, punctures for the most part clearly impressed, slightly  unevenly and moderately densely distributed and unevenly sized, the largest fractionally larger than those on head, a scatter of much finer punctures among them, punctures become coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin often almost straight for some distance, sometimes more than half, before curving smoothly towards rear margin.  Scutellum; punctured, punctures similar in size and density to those on adjacent area of pronotum, less often, punctures are larger and less well defined.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely, punctures large and distinct and relatively evenly distributed on anterior half, with much smaller punctures scattered sparingly on the interstices, many smooth, elevated mostly round verrucae, some with small central punctures are scattered over elytral surface, the most prominent of these are arranged in 3 longitudinal series, the inner one close to suture and in line with a sometimes interrupted ridge at anterior margin between scutellum and humeral callus, the outer one just inside elytral margin and confined to posterior half, the third roughly midway between these in posterior half, surface continuous under humeral callus, other verrucae and a few short laterally-oriented ridges are scattered between these, a longer such ridge is usually present just posterior to antero-median depression; posterior third of suture carinate, posterior third of marginal area prominently concave, often with a row of verrucae along its centre.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.32-0.35).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided or narrowing slightly anteriorly, open or closed anteriorly, short setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process, absent from mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, rounded.  Aedeagus; moderate-sized (LPE=32-33%BL), in dorsal view, narrowest near basal sulcus, from where sides diverge evenly anteriorly until close to anterior of ostium,  from where sides converge towards apex in a smooth but relatively tight arc, dorsal surface with shallow dorso-median channel past ostial opening, dorsolateral channels converge quite rapidly towards posterior, resulting in a tear-shaped ostium, the ostial flaps protrude forward to terminate about halfway across the broad spatula, tip small and smoothly triangular; in lateral view, curved sharply through about 45⁰ near basal sulcus then ventral surface straight all the way to apex, dorsal surface straight for about half this distance, then converging evenly towards ventral surface, tip deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta132 – this very similar species can be differentiated from Ta129 by the pronotal black markings, which in Ta129 always feature a black line on each side of discal area that runs the full length from anterior to posterior margins, where in Ta132 it is abbreviated to less than half the length from the anterior margin to the posterior.  Ta129 has at most a black spot in the foveal area, while Ta132 often has more extensive black markings there.  The antennae of males of Ta132 are shorter than those of Ta129, though in females their sizes overlap substantially.

Notes

Ta129 has been collected from coolibah, Eucalyptus coolabah (Symphyomyrtus : Adnataria).  The single specimen [2021-196] from Capella, central Queensland, fits well within the range of variation of the specimens from western Queensland.  It was obtained from an unspecified box species.
