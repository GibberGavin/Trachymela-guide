Adult Description

Length: ♀ ? mm, ♂ 5.3 mm (1).

Colour:  head: uniformly mid-brown, labrum straw-brown, dark-brown or black-tipped mandibles; antennae straw-brown throughout; pronotum: brown, same shade as head; scutellum: brown, same or fractionally darker shade as pronotum, with a slightly darker border; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with a black quadrate area straddling the elytral summit, area between scutellum, humeral callus and summit with black verrucae that give it a somewhat chequered appearance, posterior third of elytral disc similarly studded with small black verrucae with interstices dark brown, the area of dark brown interstices extending anteriorly to about humeral callus but leaving a broad band of prominently lighter brown colouring (same shade as pronotum) adjacent to the quadrate black patch at summit to its rear and sides, punctures are a fractionally darker shade than interstices throughout, which is particularly noticeable in this paler region, humeral callus very slightly darker than surrounding area; venter: mid-brown with especially margins of thoracic ventrites darker in shade, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: light scatter of light brown that leaves the black areas prominently visible.

Shape: dorsal surface almost evenly curved throughout when viewed laterally, with discontinuity between pronotum and head small, greatest height well in front of middle of elytral margin.  Head; punctures moderately large (pd~2-3xef) and relatively evenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=38.5%BL, ♀=?%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression barely discernible, surface of marginal area slightly elevated, discal area with surface smooth and only very slightly uneven, puncturation clearly impressed, individual punctures similar in size to those on head but more uniformly sized, evenly distributed and relatively dense (spacing~1.5-2.5pd), becoming much more coarse at lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and initially very gradually then more sharply towards rear margin, broadest just in front of posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; surface smooth, punctured, punctures similar in density and size to those on adjacent surface of pronotum, but less deeply impressed.  Elytra; surface evenly to very slightly unevenly curved with a slightly elevated and relatively smooth (and black) quadrate area around the elytral summit where punctures tend to be less deeply impressed than elsewhere, elsewhere punctures distinct and large, spaced at about 1.5-2 pd apart with convex interstices, evenly distributed over anterior two-third of surface, more closely spaced in posterior third, with much smaller punctures scattered sparsely on the interstices, interstices sometimes form thin transverse ridges, elytral surface very vaguely depressed just anterior to centre, verrucae are small and quite inconspicuous, present primarily in posterior third of disc and in area between scutellum, humeral callus and summit, not aligned transversely or longitudinally and most with a small central puncture, elytral suture carinate in posterior third, surface continuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.37).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel, open anteriorly, at most a very sparse scatter of setae present along prosternal process and on mesosternum, a single long seta present on each anterior, median and posterior trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=39.8%BL), in dorsal view, prominently sinuate, with two rounded notches on each side, widest near basal sulcus then narrowing gradually towards apex, the narrowing interrupted by a broad indentation near the centre and another close to the apex, just anterior to apical indentation sides converge in a smooth but tight arc towards apex, tip triangular and small, the strongly sclerotized sides enclosing the ostium are similarly sinuate, so that the unsclerotized ostial depression narrows posteriorly to just before middle of aedeagus then widens again before narrowing again towards it most posterior point, spatula quite broad, rest of dorsal surface more lightly sclerotized, dorso-median channel clearly defined, ostial flaps extend forward to a little behind spatula, ostial opening relatively small and round; in lateral view, prominently bent at about 90˚ a short distance past basal sulcus, lower surface linear on both sides of bend, with a slight downward bend near apex, tip very slightly deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta11 – this species is clearly close to Ta131, with which it shares the form of the dorsal sculpture, particularly the large, quadrate, elevated area at the elytral summit and the distribution and form of verrucae.  These species also share the basic shape of the aedeagus, although it is considerably elaborated in Ta131.  Only a single individual of Ta131 is currently available and so it is impossible to assess the extent of variation in that species.

Notes

The single individual known was collected from an unspecified ironbark.
