Ta132

Name

Trachymela sp. Ta132

Reference specimen

2007-170e, Tanami Road, Northern Territory.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 5.9-8.1 mm [8], ♂ 5.7-7.0 mm [6].

Colour:  head light- to mid-brown, usually with a pair of black patches behind fronto-clypeal suture, one on each side between eye and central suture that is very variable in size, sometimes large and even merging across central suture and extending a little into posterior of clypeus, sometimes much smaller or reduced to two darker-brown patches, less often completely absent, labrum yellow-brown, mandibles brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-5 straw-brown, 7-11 similar or grading fractionally to slightly darker; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, usually with black markings present as follows: a short black line on each side of discal area extending from close to anterior edge towards posterior edge and terminating anterior to middle of pronotum, foveal area with central black peak and another small black round mark between it and humeral callus just anterior to posterior margin, these two spots usually connected by a curved black line extending along inside edge of foveal area roughly in shape of a “c” with its open side facing laterally, sometimes some or, rarely, all of these markings are absent; scutellum: brown, same shade as adjacent area of pronotum, with lateral edges lined in a slightly darker shade;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, rarely completely so, more often with black markings as follows: a black ridge extends posteriorly from base centred between scutellum and humeral callus and in line with the black line on pronotum, sometimes this ridge extends as a black line of verrucae almost to elytral apex, with an interruption always at a depression just in front of summit, some black verrucae are scattered between it and humeral callus near its anterior end, an often broader black band parallels the first line in the posterior half of disc, about halfway between it and discal margin, other small black or dark-brown verrucae may be scattered over disc, especially along its lateral margin, a small common black area is situated across suture at its summit, punctures, humeral callus black if other black markings are present; venter: almost uniformly light- to mid-brown, thoracic ventrites sometimes slightly darker, legs sometimes paler, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Cuticular wax: grey to light-brown in colour, scattered relatively evenly and sparsely to moderately densely over head and pronotum, cover is quite light over elytra, except for a thicker layer of a paler, greyish shade in small patches (particularly adjacent to scutellum) along anterior margin, and between the verrucae in some of the longitudinally aligned series, with the latter appearing as pale dashed lines on the slightly darker background.

Shape: narrow and relatively thin, evenly rounded from scutellum to about 20% of distance from apex, where curvature increases considerably, sometimes slightly flattened around maximum height, height=39-45%BL, greatest height viewed laterally is a little in front of, or less often, quite close to middle of elytral margin.  Head; punctures small (pd~1-2xef) and relatively evenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=31-35%BL, ♀=29-32%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, usually with a noticeable explanation very close to lateral margin, foveal depression present at anterior edge with an almost round and more finely punctured or even unpunctured pustule in middle of foveal area, discal area very slightly rugulose, the anterior half of its lateral boundary sometimes in the form of a slightly raised (black) ridge, a very thin and often interrupted central longitudinal ridge often present, punctures clearly impressed and mostly evenly and densely distributed, size fractionally larger than those on head, a scatter of much finer punctures among them, punctures become coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin often almost straight for some distance before curving smoothly towards rear margin.  Scutellum; punctured, punctures similar in size and density to those on adjacent area of pronotum, less often, punctures are larger and less well defined.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely, punctures large and distinct and relatively evenly distributed on anterior half, with much smaller punctures scattered sparingly on the interstices, many smooth, elevated mostly round verrucae, some with small central punctures are scattered over elytral surface (see colour above for arrangement), surface continuous under humeral callus; posterior third of suture carinate, posterior third of marginal area prominently concave, usually with a row of widely spaced verrucae along its centre.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.35-0.38).  Venter; Prosternal process narrowest anteriorly to almost parallel-sided, open or closed anteriorly, short setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process, absent from mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, rounded; inner edge of posterior of epipleura lacking setae or with some scattered, inconspicuous ones.  Aedeagus; moderate-sized (LPE=32-35%BL), in dorsal view, sides noticeably sinuate, converging slightly and evenly from basal sulcus to about middle then diverging similarly towards widest point at about centre of ostium, from where sides converge towards apex in a smooth and gradual arc, dorsal surface with well-defined dorso-median channel and dorsolateral channels resulting in two long ostial flaps protruding forward to some distance behind spatula, ostial depression roughly oval, but posterior extent not well defined, merging into the weakly sclerotized surface behind it; tip small and smoothly triangular; in lateral view, curved smoothly through about 45⁰ between basal sulcus and middle of central section, then ventral surface almost straight to apex, dorsal surface straight a very short distance past bend, then converging evenly towards ventral surface, tip deflexed.

Similar species

Ta87 – the pattern of black markings on the elytra of this species can be remarkably like that of the very variable Ta87, and the two species are of very similar size and shape.  Ta87 differs from Ta132 in the black markings of the prothorax which consist of a black line on each side of the discal area that extends from the anterior to posterior margins, and at most a small black dot in the middle of the foveal area.  Ta87 clearly differs from Ta132 in the possession of the “ovate ostium” form of aedeagus.

Ta195 – these two species are similar in most respects, including body size and black markings when they are present.  The transverse elytral ridge, which is either absent or barely discernable in Ta132, is usually much better developed in Ta195.  The shape of the aedeagus is very similar in these two species, but that of Ta195 is 20% shorter and noticeably narrower.

Notes

This species as characterized here is quite variable in the extent of the black dorsal markings.  The most common form has the black markings as described above.  Two specimens that have no dorsal black markings at all were caught from the same Tanami Road site as several others more typical of the species.  One of these is a male and its aedeagus is the same shape as that of more typical forms of this species, while the dimensions are also those expected for a male of that size.  The two male specimens from Clermont, central Queensland, are slightly and subtly different in the shape of the aedeagus and the form of the verrucae on the elytra and may represent a separate species.

The western and central Australian specimens of Ta132 were collected from smooth-barked coolibah, Eucalyptus victrix (Symphyomyrtus : Adnataria), while the specimens from central Queensland were found on unidentified box and ironbarks species (also Symphyomyrtus : Adnataria).
