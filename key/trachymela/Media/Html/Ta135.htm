Trachymela sp. Ta135

Reference specimen

2017-218, St George, southern Queensland.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 5.8-7.6 mm, ♂ 5.4-6.5 mm.

Colour:  head mid- to dark reddish-brown, with a black patch on each side of frons extending posteriorly from fronto-clypeal suture, often the patches are narrowly joined near suture, less often they are broadly joined into a single patch across central suture, sometimes black also present along posterior of clypeus, rarely patch is absent and only fronto-clypeal suture is black, labrum yellow-brown, mandibles brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-5 straw-brown, 7-11 similar or grading fractionally to slightly darker; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with a thin dark-brown to black, longitudinal, irregular band extending almost full length of pronotum on each side behind the inside edge of eye, in a few specimens these are connected by oblique dark lines resulting in an “M”-shaped mark with its base on the posterior margin, a round darker brown or black mark is present in centre behind outside edge of each eye; scutellum: very dark brown or black;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, black ridges border the scutellum, a short, sometimes interrupted black ridge extends posteriorly from base centered between scutellum and humeral callus and continuing the black line on pronotum, many small to medium-sized black verrucae scattered over almost the whole surface, these verrucae often align  longitudinally to form series, sometimes verrucae just behind antero-median depression align to form a thin, interrupted transverse black ridge, a relatively large, longitudinal black area is present along discal margin just behind humeral callus that usually continues as evenly spaced black pustules towards elytral apex, punctures are fractionally darker than interstices, humeral callus dark brown or black; venter: head dark-brown to black, thoracic ventrites dark-brown, abdominal sternites similar or fractionally paler, inside surface of elytral epipleura black with brown along margin.  Cuticular wax: grey to light brown, relatively evenly to quite unevenly distributed over much of surface of head, pronotum and elytra, if brown, greyish streaks may sometimes be present between verrucae in row VR2 or along margin of elytral disc, greyish wax is often present at elytral base, near scutellum.

Shape: quite evenly but not very strongly rounded (height=41-47%BL), greatest height viewed laterally is in front of middle of elytral margin.  Head; punctures fine to moderate-sized (pd~1.5-2.5xef) and relatively evenly and densely packed.  Antennae short (AL ♂=35-38%BL, ♀=30-33%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, often with a noticeable explanation very close to lateral margin, foveal depression very shallow with a very marked and almost round elevated area in middle, discal area very slightly rugulose, usually bounded laterally by a slightly raised (black) and often more finely punctured ridge, punctures clearly impressed and quite evenly and densely distributed in discal area, size slightly larger than those on head, becoming coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and very gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; punctured, punctures similar in size or fractionally smaller than those on adjacent area of pronotum.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely, lightly and evenly curved longitudinally in anterior two-thirds, then curvature increases towards apex, punctures moderte in size and relatively distinct and relatively evenly distributed on anterior half, many smooth, elevated verrucae, some with small central punctures are scattered over elytral surface (see colour above for arrangement), surface continuous under humeral callus; posterior third of marginal area noticeably concave.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.36-0.40).  Venter; Prosternal process narrowest anteriorly to almost parallel-sided, closed anteriorly, short setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process, absent from mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, rounded.  Aedeagus; moderate-sized (LPE=30-35%BL), in dorsal view, sides slightly sinuate, narrowing very slightly from basal sulcus towards apex, then broadening to be widest at middle of ostium from where sides converge towards apex in a smooth arc, dorsal surface with dorso-median channel terminating at rear of ostium, two thin ostial flaps protruding towards but not reaching apex in the tear-shaped ostial depression that is slightly extended posteriorly; tip small and smoothly triangular; in lateral view, curved through about 145⁰ just past basal sulcus then ventral surface quite evenly and lightly curved almost to apex, dorsal surface parallel with ventral to rear of ostium then converging evenly towards ventral surface, tip deflexed.

Similar species

Ta126 – this species intergrades in many characters with Ta135 and the two are sometimes found together.  It is usually separable by its completely brown elytral epipleura and the fewer contrasting black markings on the prothorax and elytra – sometimes the dorsal surface is completely brown.  Ta135 is also noticeably darker ventrally than Ta126.  The aedeagus of the two species is very similar in shape though that of Ta126 tends to be very slightly shorter, more broadly rounded and wider anteriorly and the ostial opening is not extended as far posteriorly as that of Ta135.   These two species may yet prove to be variants of a single species.

Ta232 – of similar size and appearance, this species is found in similar habitats as Ta135 (sometimes together in the same location, with both species feeding on box-type eucalypts).  The inner surface of the epipleura is largely brown in specimens of Ta232 and the verrucae in series VR3 and to a lesser extent VR5 are very closely spaced, often merging to form an interrupted ridge.  VR4, VR6 and VR7 (and sometimes VR8) have quite prominent white streaks of wax between the verrucae that are absent in specimens of Ta135.  The aedeagus of these two species, though similar in size, is of a different form.

Ta87 – this slightly larger species whose range overlaps that of Ta135 in southern Queensland can be difficult to distinguish on external morphological characters.  Ta135 has wider elytral epipleura, with values of HCE/PW always above 0.36, while that value never exceeds 0.34 in Ta87.  The aedeagus of the two species, though both of the “oval” type, is quite different, with that of Ta87 very flat and broad over its apical half.

Notes

There is some variation in the arrangement of verrucae, with the Charleville specimen [25-055747] in particular having most of its verrucae in prominent longitudinal series.

Three female specimens from near Clermont, Queensland, have black epipleura and a relatively dark ventral surface as is typical for this species, but the verrucae on the dorsal surface on one or two of these specimens are quite sparse, more typical of Ta126.

Four specimens from a western Victorian location are included here.  In most respects these are well within the range of variation of specimens from northern Australia (though they all tend to be in the upper range of sizes).  The aedeagus of the only male is the same shape as those of typical Ta135, though is larger than is typical for a specimen of its length.  The cuticular wax has a greyish band along the edge of the elytral disc that is either not present or much less pronounced in typical Ta135 specimens.

Almost all specimens of Ta135 have been found on Eucalyptus species in the Adnataria group, particularly E. coolabah (Symphyomyrtus : Adnataria).  The two Charleville specimens were collected from E. camaldulensis.
