Name

Trachymela sp. Ta139

Reference specimen

2018-326, Sunset Country, 25km N of Underbool, Victoria

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 8.8-9.2 mm [3], ♂ 8.3-8.4 mm [2].

Colour:  head: mid- to reddish-brown, clypeus sometimes fractionally darker but never black, labrum straw-brown, mandibles brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-3 straw-brown, 4-11 similar or fractionally to (rarely) considerably darker brown, individual antennomeres sometimes with anterior edge darker; pronotum: brown, similar shade as head, often with with a thin, longitudinally extended darker brown patch along lateral edges of pronotal disc (behind inner edge of eye), rarely a thin dark-brown line runs longitudinally along centre, lateral edge black; scutellum: brown, slightly darker shade than adjacent area of pronotum, often broadly edged in darker brown; elytra: brown, similar shade or fractionally darker than disc of pronotum, small, very slightly paler spots are dotted over whole of disc, usually aligning in longitudinal rows, humeral callus usually darker (sometimes very slightly) than surrounding area; venter: almost uniformly dark-brown, legs of similar shade, inside surface of elytral epipleura dark-brown or black.  Cuticular wax: light-brown and somewhat unevenly distributed, with a thick coating on head and the lateral margins of pronotum; on the elytra, it is distributed more sparsely though fairly evenly, with rows of small white, dot-like patches scattered over disc and roughly aligned in irregular rows.

Shape: quite evenly rounded and almost hemispherical (H/BL ♂=0.45-0.50, ♀=0.49-0.50), highest point in front of middle of elytral margin.  Head; punctures moderately sized (pd~2-2.5xef) and quite evenly and densely packed.  Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=38-39%BL, ♀=34-35%BL), A4>A3, filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression absent or very weak with foveal elevation absent, puncturation clearly impressed, similar or slightly larger in size than those on head and similarly densely distributed and evenly distributed, becoming much coarser from edge of discal area towards lateral margin. The lateral edges of discal region sometimes slightly elevated and/or with slightly interrupted puncturation (and sometimes darker in colour), a line along centre of disc from anterior to posterior margin is similarly emphasized by slightly interrupted puncturation and (rarely) darker colouration.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially almost straight for at least 25% of length, then curving smoothly and gradually towards the much thicker rear margin.  Scutellum; surface smooth or somewhat uneven, unpunctured or with several shallow punctures.  Elytra; surface evenly curved, punctures distinct in anterior two-thirds, less so in posterior third where they become lost in the closely-spaced granular interstices, relatively evenly distributed, only very few smaller punctures scattered on the interstices, interstices raised but uniform in thickness, never expanding to form verrucae but some may join longitudinally to form thin, slightly elevated ridges, surface continuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.35-0.38).  Venter; Prosternal process narrow anteriorly, closed anteriorly, short setae present sparsely over prosternal process and mesosternum, one (rarely 2 or 3) setae on anterior and median trochanter, one on posterior trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate; posterior section of epipleura with a line of fine setae extending from opposite 2nd abdominal segment to close to apex.  Aedeagus; quite long (LPE=35-38%BL); in dorsal view, narrowing slightly but noticeably and smoothly from basal sulcus to about 2/3rds of distance to apex, from there to base of spatula the sides bulge outwards in a slight rounded arc, dorsal surface smoothly curved with a well-defined and long dorso-median channel, dorsolateral channels relatively short and along lateral margins, dorsal flaps relatively short producing a large ostial opening, spatula triangular and smoothly rounded at apex; in lateral view, curved slightly just anterior to basal sulcus, then almost straight to about 25% from apex where ventral surface bends through almost 45⁰ and its thickness decreases towards apex, tip prominent and deflexed.

Similar species

Ta124 – this species is very difficult to distinguish from Ta139 if genitalia are not available.  The species are of similar size, both lack verrucae and are mostly uniformly dark-brown dorsally and ventrally, with both having black inner epipleural surfaces.  The elytral surface texture of Ta124 tends to be more evenly granular and its colouration is more uniform, lacking the longitudinal rows of faintly paler spots.  The single specimen known of Ta124 completely lacks the faint elevated longitudinal ridges on the disc of prothorax that are present to some extent on all specimens of Ta139.

Ta76 – though similar in shape and size, this species is easily distinguishable by its uneven elytral surface, due to the presence of shallow elytral verrucae.  Ta76 also tends to have a black clypeus and its elytral suture is bordered in black.

Ta78 – distinguished by the rows of small but prominent, black verrucae on the elytra.

Notes

The species is one of a group of relatively large species found in the mallee regions of southern Australia.

No host records are available for this species.
