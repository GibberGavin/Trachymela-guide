Adult Description

Length: ♀ ? mm (0), ♂ 7.7 mm (1).

Colour:  head: frons and much of clypeus dark reddish-brown, fronto-clypeal suture and anterior margin of clypeus black, labrum black, mandibles dark-brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-3 light-brown, 4-7 grading from light-brown towards dark-brown, 8-11 very dark brown or black; pronotum: brown, same shade as head over fovealarea and along lateral margins, anterior margin between eyes broadly black, with the black extending posteriorly over surface of discal area, lateral edges partially black; scutellum: black; elytra: substantially black, with dark brown along marginal area, in two depressions along anterior edge, between scutellum and humeral callus and a larger patch near centre of antero-median depression, other small flecks of brown are scattered between the verrucae, humeral callus black; venter: black, with patches of lighter shade on abdominal ventrite V and along lateral parts of thoracic ventrites, elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: concentrated along lateral margins of pronotum and elytra and in the antero-median depression of elytra.

Shape: almost evenly curved from scutellum to apex, greatest height viewed laterally is in front of middle of elytral margin (H/BL=0.39);  Head; punctures fine (pd~1.5-2xef), rarely coarser, and quite evenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=40.0%BL, ♀=?%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression very shallow, foveal elevation clearly defined, discal area with surface slightly uneven, shiny, and puncturation clearly impressed with punctures of similar size to or slightly larger than those on head with much finer punctures interspersed between them, evenly to a little unevenly distributed and relatively dense (spacing~1.5-2pd), becoming noticeably coarser towards lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and very gradually initially, then still smoothly but more sharply towards rear margin, broadest close to posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; punctured with punctures similar in size but fractionally shallower than on adjacent area of pronotum.  Elytra; evenly curved transversely over disc, with margins noticeably explanate, antero-median depression quite small, most noticeable between VS3 and VS5; surface rough with interstices convex and thickened producing a network of dark ridges, verrucal series almost obsolete, only VS3 and VS5 clearly traceable, with VS3 elevated into a ridge close to apex, verrucae small with fine central punctures, area between suture and VR3 anterior to summit relatively smooth with finer punctures, elsewhere punctures are scattered evenly over surface but are not prominent due to rugose interstices, posterior third of elytral suture carinate, marginal sulcus well defined in posterior half, surface discontinuous under humeral callus. Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.37).  Venter; Prosternal process narrowest anteriorly, closed anteriorly, at most a very sparse scatter of setae present along prosternal process and on mesosternum, a single long seta present on anterior, median and posterior trochanters; front edge of metasternum beveled, flat.  Aedeagus; short (LPE=28.7%BL), in dorsal view, sides smoothly sinuate, widest at basal sulcus and at middle of ostium (WPE = 0.31*LPE), with narrowest point about halfway between these, from middle of ostium sides converge smoothly towards apex, tip triangular and small, dorsal surface lightly sclerotized, ostium oval, slightly longer than wide, with paired dorsal flaps projecting to spatula; in lateral view, curved about 45° at basal sulcus, ventral surface then almost straight to apex, dorsal surface parallel to ventral to about middle of ostium, from where it converges towards ventral, tip deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta165 – a rather similar species, likely of similar size and with the same type of cuticular wax distribution.  Ta165 tends to have large verrucae that are better differentiated from the network of raised interstices.  Its aedeagus is also of the “ovate ostium” type but is narrower and very prominently curved viewed laterally.

 Ta170 – almost indistinguishable from Ta140, although it is likely to be somewhat larger (but it is possible that the single specimen of Ta140 available may be an unusually small specimen).  The aedeagus of Ta170 is quite different, lacking an oval-shaped ostium.

 Ta21 – another species that is almost identical in external morphology, but may be distinguished by its black inner epipleural surfaces.

Notes

No host was recorded for the only specimen of Ta140 known.
