Distribution

Ta142

Name

Trachymela sp. Ta142

Reference specimen

2007-212b, Rabbit Flat, Tanami Rd, Northern Territory.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 5.3-5.6 mm (2), ♂ 5.3-5.8 mm (3).

Colour:  head: mid-brown, with a relatively small, round black mark in centre of frons, just behind fronto-clypeal suture, sometimes reduced to a vague fuscous spot, labrum straw-brown, mandibles brown with black tips; antennomeres uniformly straw-brown or light brown; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with a black longitudinal band along each lateral edge of disc, usually with a short oblique branch extending from it towards centre of disc, foveal area with a lateral-facing “c” shaped black mark sometimes separated at centre into two smaller marks, sometimes all black patches are joined into one large, irregular blotch, less often they are not black but of a darker brown than rest of surface, rarely are they almost completely absent; scutellum: brown, same shade as pronotum, lateral edges usually fractionally darker; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with scattered black spots and small patches that correspond to barely elevated verrucae or interstices and tend to be most concentrated in scutellar region and posterior half of disc, discal margin usually with black patches that are slightly larger and/or more concentrated, raised ridge bordering scutellum often black, a small black patch situated at elytral summit and lateral third of elytral margin sometimes black, only rarely are these black markings absent or only slightly darker than surrounding surface; punctures similar to or a slightly darker shade than interstices, humeral callus black or, less often, not black but darker than surrounding area; venter: almost uniformly light- to mid-brown, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: unknown.

Shape: almost evenly curved from scutellum to apex, or, especially in females, curvature increasing a little before apex, greatest height viewed laterally is in front of middle of elytral margin (H/BL=0.41-0.48);  Head; punctures fine (pd~1-1.5xef) and quite evenly and moderately densely distributed.  Antennae short (AL ♂=35-36%BL, ♀=33.2%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely with only extreme margins slightly explanate, foveal depression absent with epidermis slightly elevated in a small pustule behind eyes, discal area with surface mostly smooth and puncturation clearly to more vaguely impressed, punctures similar in size or fractionally larger than those on head, evenly distributed and moderately dense (spacing~2-3pd), becoming much more coarse at lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially curving smoothly and very gradually towards rear margin, curvature increasing towards rear margin, broadest just in front of posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; variable, most often densely punctured, with punctures similar in size to those on disc of pronotum, less often unpunctured, but surface rough.  Elytra; surface evenly to slightly unevenly curved, punctures distinct and relatively large, closely spaced and evenly distributed over anterior two-third of surface, more closely spaced towards apex, with much smaller punctures scattered on the interstices, verrucae if present are small and barely elevated above level of interstices and quite sparsely distributed over surface of disc, with VS2 and VS3 sometimes forming shallow ridges close to apex, elytral suture carinate in posterior third, surface continuous under humeral callus, margin almost straight with anterior section usually slightly oblique.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.36-0.38).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel, closed anteriorly, a sparse scatter of setae present along prosternal process and on mesosternum, a single long seta present on anterior, median and posterior trochanters; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; short (LPE=25-27%BL), lanceolate, in dorsal view, widest at basal sulcus with sides initially converging quite gradually (sometimes almost parallel) and uniformly for about two-thirds the distance to apex, from where rate of convergence increases, sometimes becoming slightly uneven, to apex, dorsal surface very membranous, ostium relatively large and in shape of a thin triangle, tip small and triangular; in lateral view, bent at 150˚ near basal sulcus, lower edge towards apex from bend straight to apex, dorsal surface roughly parallel to ventral surface from basal sulcus for 60% of distance to apex, then converging linearly towards it, deflexed, flagellum moderately thick and straight on exit.

Similar species

 Ta185 – a species that occurs in the same part of the country that also can have have quite inconspicuous verrucae.  It lacks black markings on the prothorax and the elytral verrucae, though small, are more clearly distinguished from the interstices, being round and well elevated.

 Ta193 – another species with inconspicuous verrucae.  It completely lacks any dorsal black colour and its elytral surface is never as smooth as that of Ta142, with interstices noticeably more convex.

Notes

Ta142 has been recorded from Corymbia candida.
