Name

Trachymela sp. Ta143

Reference specimen

2003-180, Blackdown Tableland, central Queensland.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 8.3-8.5 mm (2), ♂ 8.1 mm (1).

Colour:  head mid- to dark-brown, sometimes with some darker or black along front-clypeal suture, labrum straw-brown, mandibles brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-4 light to mid-brown, 5-7 similar or grading darker, 8-11 mid to dark brown, individual antennomeres in distal section of antenna often darker in distal half; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with four small, circular darker brown to almost black marks, one behind inside edge of each eye, the other near middle of foveal area; scutellum: brown, similar to adjacent area of pronotum, broadly bordered laterally in darker brown;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with prominent black raised, shiny, mostly round smooth pustules studding the whole surface, the most prominent associated with VR3 and VR5, the latter usually with the largest of these situated in the middle of the disc in its posterior quarter, a higher concentration of these black pustules is present just behind antero-median depression, usually VR2 and sometimes VR3 have relatively small but more closely spaced black pustules near and just behind elytral summit, another line of often evenly spaced small black pustules runs from black median marginal patch towards apex along centre of marginal sulcus; punctures are similar in colour or fractionally darker than interstices, humeral callus black; venter: dark-brown, usually tending towards a lighter brown shade towards lateral extremes, legs dark-brown, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: light brown, somewhat unevenly distributed, with densest coverage around antero-median depression, sometimes denser patches are roughly arranged in longitudinal rows, sometimes faint thin interrupted rows of greyish colour are present, particularly between the verrucae in VR3 and VR4.

Shape: relatively flat (H=40-43%BL), evenly and lightly rounded from scutellum to close to apex, where curvature increases slightly but noticeably, greatest height viewed laterally is a little anterior to middle of elytral margin; Head; punctures relatively small (pd~1-1.5xef), evenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=44.2%BL, ♀=33-35%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression very shallow or absent with a clearly defined and almost round elevated area in middle, disc with punctures clearly impressed, fractionally larger than on head and evenly and moderately densely distributed, sometimes with a small less densely punctured region on each side, punctures become coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins, interstices relatively smooth in discal area.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially almost straight for a little less than half its length, then curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; smooth and unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely, a distinct but shallow depression extends across surface just in front of middle, punctures moderately large and distinct and quite evenly distributed where not interrupted by the prominent verrucae, verrucae vary from small to moderately large, not regularly arranged within their series so have the appearance of being scattered irregularly over the discal surface, see colour above for distribution, elytral suture not carinate, surface discontinuous under humeral callus, marginal area in posterior half prominently sulcate, lateral margin straight in posterior ¾, slightly oblique in anterior ¼..  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.34-0.36).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided, lightly closed anteriorly, setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; short (LPE=24.7%BL), in dorsal view, sides smoothly sinuous, narrowing very slightly from basal sulcus to middle then broadening to its widest point near apical extreme of ostium, before sides converge towards apex in a smooth arc, dorsal surface lightly sclerotized with dorso-median channel faint, short but well-defined dorsolateral channels present adjacent to ostium; ostium approximately quadrate, dorsal flaps extend to near its its apical edge when extended with each having an inward-facing semicircular indentation near its apex, tip protruding and truncate; in lateral view, almost evenly and lightly curved throughout with curvature at basal sulcus slightly greater, thinning towards apex over 15% of length, tip strongly deflexed.

Similar species

Notes
