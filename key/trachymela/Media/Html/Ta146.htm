Name

Trachymela sp. Ta146

Reference specimen

2017-175, Quorn, South Australia.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 7.0-7.9 mm [4], ♂ 6.6-7.5 mm [8].

Colour:  head mid- to dark brown, often uniformly so, sometimes with vaguely darker (rarely almost black) patches on clypeus and along fronto-clypeal suture, labrum yellow-brown, mandibles with black tips; antennomeres 1-5 straw-brown, 7-11 similar or, more often, grading to darker brown, rarely grading to black; pronotum: brown, same shade or slightly darker than head, most often completely so, rarely with a small black mark on each side of disc, near anterior margin; scutellum: very dark brown with lateral margins fractionally darker, or, very rarely, black;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, often uniformly so throughout, including the many scattered small verrucae and small elevated transverse ridges, sometimes many or all of the verrucae and ridges are darker brown or, less often, black, punctures fractionally darker than interstices, humeral callus variable, concolourous with surrounds or darker brown or, rarely, black; venter: mostly dark-brown or black, with a few lighter-coloured patches sometimes present, legs similar or fractionally darker, abdominal sternites similar, inside surface of elytral epipleura variable, commonly black or mostly black, but sometimes completely brown.  Cuticular wax: light-brown or greyish brown in colour, and very patchily distributed over much of surface of head, pronotum and elytra; lateral margins of pronotum and elytra generally with a slightly denser coverage, antero-median depression sometimes with a slightly thicker layer, occasionally the distribution on posterior half of elytral disc has small clumps that line up in several longitudinal series.

Shape: almost evenly rounded from scutellum to apex, greatest height viewed laterally is a little in front of middle of elytral margin.  Head; punctures moderately sized (pd~1.5-2.5xef) and relatively evenly and densely packed.  Antennae short (AL ♂=39-43%BL, ♀=32.7%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression absent, a small and roughly round elevated area present directly behind middle of each eye, marginal area often noticeably raised above curvature of pronotum, discal area quite smooth, sometimes with a fractionally raised area present as a broad ridge from center extending obliquely forward toward lateral edge of disc, punctures clearly impressed and quite evenly and densely distributed in discal area, average size fractionally larger than those on head, but a range of sizes are intermingled, punctures becoming coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and very gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; punctured, punctures similar-sized or fractionally smaller than those on adjacent area of pronotum.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely, surface quite variable, sometimes punctures moderately-sized and distinct in anterior half among many discrete verrucae, or punctures much less distinct with surface a jumbled mix of elevated interstices interspersed with verrucae that are only slightly more elevated, verrucae, often with small central punctures, usually without regular arrangement, but often slightly more concentrated just posterior to antero-median depression and some may align in longitudinal series in posterior half of disc, are scattered over elytral surface (see colour above for arrangement), suture carinate in posterior third, lateral margin almost straight, sometimes with apex extended down slightly, surface continuous under humeral callus, vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.34-0.40), inner edge lightly scattered with short setae posteriorly.  Venter; Prosternal process narrowest anteriorly to almost parallel-sided, closed (rarely open) anteriorly, short setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process, absent from mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, rounded; posterior section of epipleura sparsely scattered with short setae, rarely almost absent.  Aedeagus; relatively short (LPE=27-31%BL), in dorsal view, almost parallel-sided or narrowing fractionally from basal sulcus to rear of ostium, then sides diverge slightly to widest point just behind front of ostium, from where sides converge towards apex in a smooth arc, dorsal surface with well-defined dorso-median channel and dorsolateral channels resulting in two long ostial flaps protruding forward almost to spatula in the slightly elongated, oval ostial depression; tip small and smoothly triangular; in lateral view, curved sharply through about 45⁰ near basal sulcus then ventral surface almost straight to 20% of distance from apex, where it curves downward noticeably, dorsal surface straight for about same distance, then converging evenly towards ventral surface, tip deflexed.

Notes

Ta146 has been found on box-barked eucalypts, particularly Eucalyptus porosa (Symphyomyrtus : Adnataria).

Specimens of Ta146 have been collected from two localities in South Australia, Quorn in the Flinders Ranges and Cowell and Mangalo on the Eyre Peninsula.  The male specimens from the Eyre Peninsula have a very slightly longer aedeagus and fractionally longer antennae.  The Eyre Peninsula specimens also tend to have a more ruggedly sculptured elytral surface, this being especially marked in the area just posterior to the antero-median depression and their verrucae and raised interstices are darker in colour than those of the typical Flinders Ranges specimens.  In addition, all Eyre Peninsula specimens have the inner epipleural surface brown, whereas the Flinders Ranges specimens include examples that range from completely black to completely brown.  These differences are most likely accounted for by geographic variation, but there is a possibility that two species are being represented here.
