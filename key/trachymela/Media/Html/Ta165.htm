Name

Trachymela sp. Ta165

Reference specimen

2017-556a, Boonoo Boonoo, N.E. New South Wales

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 7.5-7.9 mm (2), ♂ 6.4-7.7 mm (4).

Colour:  head: uniformly mid- to dark-brown, front margin just behind labrum often darker (even black), mandibles dark-brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-3 light-brown, 4-7 grading from light-brown towards dark-brown, 8-11 very dark brown or black; pronotum: brown, same shade or fractionally darker than head, sometimes with shade varying very slightly over surface, a dark-brown or black, oval spot often located on either side of centre, small black elevated peak sometimes present in foveal area, lateral edges usually at least partially black; scutellum: very dark brown or, more usually, black; elytra: brown, same shade as darkest region of pronotum, scattered with many mostly small, black verrucae in a seemingly random arrangement except for 2 or 3 rows of these verrucae that are arranged in usually inconspicuous series, the most prominent series continues a short black ridge from anterior margin midway between scutellum and humeral callus, the others are spaced between it and discal margin, rarely are these series more conspicuous than the other verrucae, posterior third of elytral suture sometimes black; punctures are similar to or a fractionally darker shade than interstices, humeral callus black; venter: black, with abdominal ventrites and distal leg segments sometimes a slightly lighter shade, elytral epipleura completely or partially black.  Waxy dorsal covering: light brown to orange-brown, with a very patchy distribution - concentrations are present on the head, on marginal area of pronotum surrounding but excluding the foveal peak and especially along lateral margins, in an uneven band along lateral margins of elytra (in the sub-marginal sulcus) and 2 small patches at base of each elytron,  smaller patches are arranged in 3 longitudinal lines from antero-median depression towards apex; elsewhere the dust is very thinly scattered and may be of a lighter shade.

Head; punctures fine (pd~1-1.5xef), rarely coarser, and slightly unevenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=41-43%BL, ♀=32-34%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression absent or weak, foveal elevation clearly defined, discal area with surface slightly uneven, shiny, and puncturation clearly impressed with punctures of similar size to or slightly larger than those on head, evenly to very slightly unevenly distributed and relatively dense (spacing~1.5-2pd), becoming noticeably coarser towards lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin, broadest very close to posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; punctured with punctures similar in size but shallower than on adjacent area of pronotum.  Elytra; viewed laterally, curvature of dorsal outline slight and even over anterior half, then increasing noticeably in posterior half, antero-median depression very slight; almost evenly curved transversely, surface rough with interstices convex and thickened producing a network of often dark ridges, mostly small, elevated verrucae, some with a fine central puncture are arranged as described in “colour” section, punctures are scattered evenly over surface but are not prominent due to rugose interstices, usually more clearly defined but smaller in a small smooth area straddling the elytral summit, posterior half of marginal area convex, surface discontinuous under humeral callus. Vertical extension of epipleura relatively small (HCE/PW=0.32-0.36).  Venter; Prosternal process narrowest anteriorly, widening slowly posteriorly, closed anteriorly, at most a very sparse scatter of setae present along prosternal process and on mesosternum, a single long seta present on anterior, median and posterior trochanters; front edge of metasternum beveled, flat.  Aedeagus; short (LPE=30-34%BL), in dorsal view, approximately parallel-sided from base then widening at ostium to maximum width in anterior half of ostium  (WPE = 0.25-0.27*LPE) from where sides converge smoothly towards apex, tip triangular and small, dorsal surface lightly sclerotized, dorso-median channel present but very faint past ostium, ostium oval with paired dorsal flaps projecting more than ¾ of distance into it; in lateral view, moderately curved near base, then curvature smaller but even over whole length to apex, tip deflexed, flagellum thin and straight.

Similar species

 Ta21 – small specimens of this species can be very difficult to separate from specimens of Ta165 without access to the aedeagus; this issue is discussed in some detail in the discussion of similar species to Ta21.

 Ta113 – the distribution of cuticular wax in this species is of the typical subsutus-group type and thus very similar to that of Ta165.  The two species also share similar body sizes and an aedeagus with ovate ostium.  They differ in that the clypeus of Ta113 is black, the verrucae of Ta113 are less prominent than those of Ta165 and the vertical epipleural extension in Ta113 is much larger (HCE/PW=0.39-0.40).  The aedeagus of Ta113 is less slender and slightly shorter, though there is some overlap in the latter character.

Notes

Ta165 is relatively uncommonly collected compared with the much more abundant Ta21.  It tends to occur on similar hosts to Ta21, having been collected from smooth-barked eucalypts including Eucalyptus deanei and E. blakelyi.
