Name

Trachymela sp. Ta166

Reference specimen

2018-002b, Liston, N.E. New South Wales

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 8.8-9.2 mm (6), ♂ 7.9-8.8 mm.

Colour:  head: very dark reddish-brown, with extensive patches of black anterior to fronto-clypeal suture and often extending posteriorly from there, sometimes completely black, mandibles mostly black; antennomeres 1-2 light-brown, 3-4 grading from light-brown to black, 5-11 black; pronotum: black, sometimes with lateral margins, less often anterior margin and rarely, posterior margin dark reddish-brown; scutellum: black; elytra: black, sometimes completely so, but usually with some parts of marginal area and small longitudinally aligned patches in 2 or 3 rows along centre of disc reddish-brown; venter: black, epipleura black.  Waxy dorsal covering: brown, a very sparse sprinkle over most of surface with much denser concentrations along the lateral margins of both prothorax and elytra, on either side of midline of head and dotted along two (sometimes a partial third) bands running across the centre of disc of elytra.

Shape:  quite evenly rounded but relatively flat, highest point about halfway along elytral margin, ventrites project a short distance below elytral epipleura.  Head; punctures moderate in size (pd~1.5-2xef) and quite evenly but densely distributed.  Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=40-42%BL, ♀=34-36%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; evenly curved transversely over discal region, lateral margins slightly explanate, sometimes very slightly, with foveal depression very shallow or even absent though always with a slight rounded elevation near its centre, puncturation always clearly impressed, evenly and quite densely distributed in discal area with a mix of puncture sizes usually present, the larger fractionally larger than those on head, becoming much coarser at lateral margins.  Front margins join the thinner lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin straight for a very short distance then curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin, becoming noticeably thicker posteriorly.  Scutellum; smooth, though surface slightly wrinkled, unpunctured.  Elytra; surface slightly flattened in front of highest point, punctures relatively evenly distributed but distinct only in a strip adjacent to suture from scutellum to about summit, elsewhere obscured by the network of thickened interstices, with much smaller punctures scattered very sparingly on the interstices, interstices are prominently raised to form a network of ridges and wrinkles over the whole surface of disc, with a few slightly thicker ones appearing as inconspicuous verrucae scattered among them, posterior third of marginal area prominently concave, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura small (HCE/PW=0.29-0.32).  Venter; Prosternal process variable narrowest anteriorly or almost parallel-sided, lightly closed anteriorly, short setae present at rear of prosternal process and on mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=29-31%BL), in dorsal view, sides parallel from base to about 15-20% of distance from apex, from there sides converge towards apex initially gradually, then more rapidly, dorsal surface smoothly curved with dorso-median channel present, though sometimes very faint, dorsolateral channels well-defined, tip prominent but truncate; in lateral view, almost evenly curved throughout most of length, then a pronounced (about 30°) downward bend shortly before apex, thinning towards apex over about 15-20% of length, tip strongly deflexed, flagellum thick, flattened and deeply grooved dorsally, thinning rapidly.

Similar species

 Ta217 - this species is very similar in external structural features and colouring to Ta166 but it differs in the smaller body length of individuals and the different distribution and colours of the cuticular wax.  The aedeagus of the two species are similar structurally but that of Ta217 is more strongly bent downwards (in lateral view) as that of Ta166.

 Ta20 – this species has a similar shape as Ta166, with greatest height either at or a little behind centre of elytral margin.  Its elytral surface is smoother, lacking the small verrucae that extend some way above the level of the interstices in Ta166.  Most specimens of Ta20 have lighter-coloured longitudinal semi-continuous interstices on the elytra and a very even distribution of waxy dust.

 Ta23 – the pattern of distribution of dust on the elytra tends to be quite similar in this species, while the sculpture of the elytra is similar in this species and variety Ta23b.  They may be distinguished by the much lighter colouring of Ta23 ventrally, especially the brown rather than black epipleura.

Notes

One of those species of Trachymela where the “greatest height” of the insect is at or behind the middle of the elytral margin. 

Ta166 has been collected from Eucalyptus blakelyi, E. prava and unspecified smooth-barked and box-barked eucalypts.
