Distribution

Ta168

Name

Trachymela sp. Ta168

Reference specimen

2001-232, Blackheath, Blue Mountains, N.S.W.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 8.0-9.2 mm, ♂ 8.2-8.4 mm.

Colour:  head: uniformly light- to mid-brown, mandibles with black tips; antennomeres 1-3 straw-brown, 4-6 grading towards darker brown, segments with proximal extreme usually slightly paler 7-11 dark brown; pronotum: uniformly brown, same shade as head; scutellum: brown, similar shade as pronotum, sometimes broadly bordered in a fractionally darker shade; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with a scatter of small, dark-brown verrucae distributed very evenly over much of disc (density of these in antero-median depression is usually slightly lower), most of the verrucae are placed within longitudinal rows, punctures slightly darker-brown, humeral callus same colour as surrounds; venter: mid-brown, with edges of ventrites sometimes slightly darker, legs similar in shade, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: light-brown in colour and evenly and sparsely distributed over dorsal surface.

Shape: evenly rounded, greatest height just in front of centre of elytra, often (especially males) noticeably explanate.

Head; punctures moderately sized (pd~2-2.5xef) and quite evenly and densely packed, a small unpunctured space is sometimes present at rear of head between eyes.  Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=42-43%BL, ♀=34-38%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely over disc with lateral margins noticeably explanate, foveal depression shallow but well-developed, central elevation absent, puncturation clearly and deeply impressed and noticeably coarser than that on head, punctures relatively evenly distributed and moderately dense in discal area (spacing=1.5-2pd) interspersed with a few small, slightly elevated and more sparsely punctured areas, interstices sometimes quite elevated producing a rough surface, punctures becoming much coarser towards lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially roughly straight for about half of length, sometimes slightly undulate, before curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; unpunctured, the flat rough-surfaced lateral borders contrast with the domed and smooth central region.  Elytra; surface evenly curved with a very shallow antero-median depression, punctures distinct and relatively evenly distributed except close to apex, with much smaller punctures scattered sparsely on the interstices, interstices form a prominent network of elevated ridges around the punctures, small round but quite prominent and evenly spaced verrucae are present over most of disc and arranged within longitudinal rows, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura large (HCE/PW=0.39-0.42).  Venter; Prosternal process parallel sided for a distance before widening, lightly closed anteriorly, short setae present sparsely over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=31-33%BL), in dorsal view, narrowing slightly and briefly from base then almost parallel sided for much of length, sides slightly undulate around ostium with widest point at base of spatula, from where the sides converge rapidly towards apex in a slightly convex arc, dorsal surface smoothly curved with a relatively long dorso-median channel, dorsolateral channels long to produce very long dorsal flaps, tip protruding moderately, ostial opening large; in lateral view, almost evenly and very gently curved throughout its length, thinning towards apex only over final 10% of length, tip prominent and deflexed.

Similar species

Ta42 – this species is very similar in general appearance but is easily distinguished by its possession of a posterior trochanter spur.

Ta02 – a superficially similar species, the distribution of verrucae on its elytra is much less regular and it has a black humeral callus and the ventral surface of head is at least partially black.

Notes

Ta168 has been recorded from unspecified smooth-barked Eucalyptus species, with the single record from the northern tablelands from the peppermint E. acaciiformis.
