Ta17

Name

Trachymela sp. Ta17

Reference specimen

2000-259, Boonoo Boonoo, N.E. New South Wales.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 5.4-7.1 mm, ♂ 5.8-6.5 mm.

Colour:  head: uniformly orange-brown to red-brown with black-tipped mandibles; antennomeres 1-4 light or straw-brown, 5-8 grading from that shade to dark-brown, 9-11 dark-brown or (rarely) black; pronotum: brown, same shade as head; scutellum: similar shade or fractionally darker than pronotum, if same shade, may be edged in fractionally darker brown; elytra: brown, same shade or fractionally darker than pronotum, a slightly or considerably darker (rarely black) and approximately rectangular area straddles the elytral summit, slightly darker patches may be present on disc, verrucae usually concolourous, rarely slightly darker, than surrounding area, humeral callus concolourous with surrounds; venter: variable, gula of head black, prothoracic ventrite often black, lateral areas of thorax and abdomen light- to mid-brown, central areas are darker brown, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown or black.  Cuticular wax: light brown of uniform shade, distributed unevenly over surface, the quadrate area around elytral summit is dust-free and usually bordered by the highest concentration of dust.

 Head; punctures relatively large (pd~2-2.5xef) and somewhat unevenly and moderately densely packed, interstices sometimes conspicuously elevated. Antennae relatively long (AL ♂=44-48%BL, ♀=39-42%BL), distal segment very slightly expanded and a little flattened.  Pronotum; disc evenly curved transversely with a broad but very shallow depression situated behind the outside edge of each eye, discal surface usually slightly rugose, rarely smooth, puncturation clearly impressed, quite evenly distributed and moderately dense in discal area, becoming considerably coarser at lateral margins.  Front margins join the slightly thinner lateral margin in a smoothly rounded right-angle, lateral margin continues very briefly in a straight line then curve smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; usually smooth and shiny, unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely with a shallow depression near middle of disc anterior to mid-line, elytral surface has a rough texture with the large punctures often lost among the raised interstices that near the scutellum form two or three longitudinal ridges, isolated verrucae with one or more small punctures are mostly confined to posterior third of disc and may be pustular or slightly extended longitudinally, transverse wrinkles of raised interstices are sometimes present just behind elytral depression, a raised, quadrate and relatively smooth and flat surface is present around elytral summit and is usually traversed by rows of punctures, elytral suture not carinate, surface slightly discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.32-0.37).  Venter; Prosternal process narrow anteriorly, broadening towards posterior, lightly closed anteriorly, short setae present at rear of prosternal process, absent from mesosternum, 1-3 setae present on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; long (LPE=33-36%BL), lanceolate, in dorsal view, almost parallel-sided for most of central section, then sides converging gradually from about 40% of length from apex, dorsal surface smoothly curved with very faint dorso-median channel usually present, dorsolateral channels short, tip protruding slightly; in lateral view, almost evenly curved over most of length, thinning towards apex over final 20%, tip deflexed, flagellum thin and almost invariably broken off.

Similar species

 Ta56 – shares with Ta17 the black gula on head and the quadrate, raised area straddling the summit of elytral suture and the rough texture of the elytra.  However, Ta56 is larger, and has a much longer adeagus.

 Ta11 – similar in possession of a quadrate, dust-free, black area straddling elytral summit, but differs in its smaller size, the lack of a black gula and in having a very different shape of aedeagus.

 Ta05 – superficially similar, but lacks the quadrate flat area around elytral summit

Notes

The sex ratio is strongly female-biased, with 16 of 19 specimens examined (84%) being female (p<0.01 using an exact binomial test).

 This species is not often collected.  It is relatively easy to identify by the large, dust-free quadrate area around the elytral summit.  The inner surface of the epipleura is black in specimens from the northern NSW highlands, but brown in specimens from Victoria.  Ta17 is most commonly found on stringybark, though a few specimens have also been recorded from Eucalyptus andrewsii, E. obliqua and unspecified peppermints.

[2018-085] has the quadrate area around the elytral very sharply defined by a broad verruca-free border.  It also differs from typical Ta17 by the different texture of the rest of the elytral surface.

[COL-01445], a female from Stanthorpe, is almost certainly different.  Though similar in size to Ta17 and with the texture of the dorsal surface roughly like that species, its verrucae are smaller, and it lacks the quadrate flat area at the elytral summit.  Its elytral epipleura are completely brown, which would be unusual for a specimen of Ta17 from this locality.
