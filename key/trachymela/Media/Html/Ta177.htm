Name

Trachymela karattae (Blackburn)

Reference specimen

2018-270a, Stokes Bay, Kangaroo Island, South Australia

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 10.2-10.8 mm (2), ♂ 10.3 mm (1).

Colour:  head: uniformly mid-brown with black-tipped mandibles, lateral margin of clypeus narrowly black; antennomeres 1-4 light-brown, 8-11 dark-brown or (less commonly) black, intermediate segments grade between these shades; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with two small to moderate-sized, black, round patches, one behind each eye, sometimes vague dark or black areas also around lateral edge of discal area; scutellum: brown, same shade as pronotum, usually broadly edged in a slightly darker shade of brown; elytra: uniformly brown, same shade as pronotum, small dark or black verrucae of approximately uniform size are always present, arranged in longitudinal series from closely to quite widely-spaced, elytral suture from scutellum to elytral summit is black, the black sometimes extending as far as about VR3, area between humeral callus and anterior edge of elytron is black, the black colouring usually ending at highest point of callus; venter: dark-brown to black, with legs a paler shade, inner surface of epipleura brown.  Cuticular wax: a sparse to moderate layer of uniform shade that can be from light grey to brown.

Head; punctures relatively large (pd~2xef) and quite evenly and densely packed, rarely with some rugose interstices. Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=54.9%BL, ♀=38.7%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; evenly curved transversely over disc, then becoming explanate towards margins producing a broad shallow depression behind the outside edge of each eye, puncturation clearly impressed, quite evenly and densely distributed in discal area, becoming considerably coarser at lateral margins.  Fine interstitial punctures are absent.  Front margins join lateral margins in a very smoothly rounded right-angle, anterior half of lateral margin almost straight with posterior half then curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; unpunctured, surface usually rough, but can be almost smooth.  Elytra; discal surface with a broad, transverse, shallow depression just in front of middle, punctures distinct and quite evenly distributed, the interstices forming a network of ridges that can include some oblique and even transverse ones, many small pimple-like raised verrucae, some with a central puncture, are arranged longitudinally and usually widely-spaced in about 9 rows, VR3 usually present as a short, raised ridge between anterior edge and post-basal depression, surface discontinuous (depressed) under humeral callus.  Epipleura sometimes conspicuously explanate, their vertical extension considerable (HCE/PW=0.39-0.40).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided, lightly closed anteriorly, short setae present at rear of prosternal process, absent from mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate, basal tarsal segment of front and mid legs in male very large, broadly elliptical; inner edge of epipleura devoid of setae.  Aedeagus; large (LPE=44.5%BL), in dorsal view, widest at base of central section then narrowing towards apex, initially very gradually then more noticeably from about 25% of distance from apex before the sides converge towards apex to form a blunt triangle, dorsal surface smoothly curved with dorso-median channel usually present but not clearly defined, dorsolateral channels present and quite well defined, tip triangular, ostium longer than broad; in lateral view, a noticeable bend near base of central section, then evenly and very slightly curved through most of length with curvature increasing close to apex, thinning towards apex over 20% of length, tip deflexed.

Similar species

Ta68 – this species is almost identical in size and structure, but has rounder pronotal front angles, a generally broader body shape and the elytral verrucae are not as clearly placed in longitudinal series as in Ta177.  The shape and dimensions of the aedeagus are almost identical.

Notes

Identification as Trachymela karattae is by comparison with the type in the BMNH.

Ta177 has been collected from Eucalyptus cosmophylla.
