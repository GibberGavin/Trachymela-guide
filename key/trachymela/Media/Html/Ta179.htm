Name

Trachymela sp. Ta179

Reference specimen

2007-215d, Halls Creek, north Western Australia.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 6.3-7.2 mm (7), ♂ 6.1-6.5 mm (5).

Colour:  head: uniformly yellow-brown to mid-brown, dark-brown or black-tipped mandibles; antennomeres uniformly straw-brown; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with lateral marginal area fractionally lighter shade; scutellum: brown, same shade as pronotum, with margins of fractionally darker shade; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, sometimes with fractionally darker shade in area between scutellum, humeral callus and elytral summit; punctures are similar to or a slightly darker shade than interstices, humeral callus same shade as surrounding area; venter: yellowish-brown with metaventrites sometimes fractionally darker in shade, legs straw-brown, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: unknown, likely to be similar to Ta130.

Shape: very slightly gibbous in lateral view, greatest height viewed laterally well in front of middle of elytral margin, dorsal surface with noticeable discontinuity in curvature between pronotum and elytra;  Head; punctures moderately to very large (pd~2-4xef), often quite unevenly distributed, interstices sometimes elevated and rough, especially near eyes, with some prominent longitudinally-oriented ridges present. Antennae short, particularly in females (AL ♂=39-42%BL, ♀=33%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression absent with epidermis elevated in a small pustule behind eyes, discal area with surface quite uneven and puncturation sometimes clearly but more often very vaguely impressed, an elevated pustule is present on each side of disc, near its lateral margin, puncture sizes vary considerably and are unevenly distributed and moderately dense (spacing~2-4pd), becoming much more coarse at lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a very smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially and very briefly straight before curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin, broadest just in front of posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; punctured, with punctures similar in size but often better defined than on pronotum, evenly or unevenly distributed.  Elytra; surface evenly to slightly unevenly curved, punctures distinct and relatively large, relatively evenly distributed over anterior two-third of surface, surface slightly more granular in posterior third, with a sparse scatter of much smaller punctures on interstices, all but posterior third of disc is scattered with mostly short transverse, elevated ridges, with one just behind the shallow and usually small antero-median depression sometimes slightly longer, some small round verrucae present in posterior third of disc, sometimes aligned in longitudinal series or, less often, lightly merged to form a longitudinal ridge, surface continuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.37-0.41).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel, open or lightly closed anteriorly, a sparse scatter of setae present along prosternal process and on mesosternum, a single long seta present on median and posterior trochanters, usually 1, rarely 2, on anterior trochanters; front edge of metasternum beveled, slightly rounded.  Aedeagus; relatively long and thin (LPE=30-33%BL, WPE ~ 0.22*LPE), in dorsal view, sides strongly sinuous, narrowing from basal sulcus towards middle and then widening again to the widest point just behind middle of ostium from where sides converge smoothly towards apex, the rate of convergence increasing sharply just before apex, tip triangular, ostium oval, considerably longer than wide, dorso-median channel short, sometimes extending very faintly a little past ostium, dorsolateral channels short; dorsal flaps extend almost to anterior of ostium; in lateral view, curved gently through about 60° from posterior extreme to a little past basal sulcus, then almost straight to apex, tip deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta130 – this species is very similar to Ta179 but can usually be distinguished by the transverse ridge across the middle of the elytral disc, which is clearly longer and distinct from the transverse wrinkles that cover much of the rest of the elytral surface.  The aedeagus of Ta179 has a more distinct expansion at the level of the ostium, and the narrowing from there to the apex is more extended.

Notes

Ta130 has been collected from the ‘box’ species Eucalyptus coolabah, E. victrix and E. tectifica  (all Symphyomyrtus : Adnataria).
