Ta18

Name

Trachymela costipennis (Chapuis)

Reference specimen

2003-307, Guyra, NSW

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 8.1-9.3 mm, ♂ 7.8-8.9 mm.

Colour:  head uniformly mid- to dark-brown with black-tipped mandibles; labrum sometimes slightly paler; antennomeres 1-4 light to mid-brown, 5-11 grading darker towards apex, rarely much darker or even black; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with two black patches present on each side, the larger one is close to the centre and roughly oval in shape, situated obliquely with its inner edge closer to the front, the smaller one is circular and situated behind eye, the inner patches are sometimes merged into one large black central patch; scutellum: dark-brown, darker shade than adjacent area of pronotum;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with raised black longitudinally extended ridges arranged over the whole surface as 9-10 more or less interrupted rows, punctures are similar or fractionally darker than interstices, humeral callus black; venter: completely black, rarely tending dark-brown at lateral extremes, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown, rarely tending darker.  Cuticular wax: moderate to thick layer of grey-brown to brown, with different shades present on most individuals, that do not conceal the dark verrucae.

Shape: rather flattened, greatest height viewed laterally is at around middle of elytral margin; Head; punctures moderately sized (pd~1.5-2xef), sometimes slightly larger posteriorly, evenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=40-42%BL, ♀=31-33%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression very shallow or absent with a clearly defined and almost round elevated area in middle, puncturation clearly impressed and quite evenly and moderately densely distributed in discal area, becoming coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins, interstices usually smooth in discal area, less often rough but never so as to obscure punctures.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; smooth and unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely, lightly curved longitudinally in anterior half, then slightly more strongly curved, a distinct but shallow depression extends across surface just in front of middle, punctures large and distinct and relatively evenly distributed between the prominent verrucae, verrucae form interrupted longitudinal series with the individual verrucae also tending to be much longer than wide, elytral suture not carinate, surface somewhat discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.32-0.38).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided, lightly closed (rarely open) anteriorly, short setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior, median and posterior trochanters; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate; inner edge of posterior of epipleurae lacking setae.  Aedeagus; short (LPE=24-27%BL), in dorsal view, almost parallel-sided initially then broadening very slightly before sides converge towards apex in a smooth arc, dorsal surface lightly sclerotized with faint dorso-median channel present, short but well defined dorsolateral channels present adjacent to ostium; tip broad, protruding and truncate, ostium short, roughly oval, posterior shaped as a deep triangle with apex at centre; in lateral view, almost evenly curved throughout basal and central section, thinning towards apex over 15% of length, tip deflexed, flagellum thick where it exits ostium.

Similar species

 Ta22 - this species co-occurs with Ta18 over a broad range.  It is superficially similar but differs by the black verrucae not being as prominently elongated and arranged in longitudinal series.  The aedeagus of Ta22 is larger and more prominently rounded.  Ta22 also tends to be associated with the peppermint group of eucalypts.

Notes

The identification of this species as T. costipennis (Chapuis) is by comparison with the male holotype in the RBINS.

The form of the elongated verrucae does not seem to vary much in specimens over its whole range and serves to distinguish the species from its close relatives.  Ta18 is distributed in the New England Tablelands of northern NSW and the adjacent areas of Queensland.  It has been recorded almost exclusively from the stringybark group of Eucalyptus (Eucalyptus : Capillulus), with the single exception being a specimen found on E. pauciflora.
