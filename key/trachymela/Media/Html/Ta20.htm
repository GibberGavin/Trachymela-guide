Distribution

Ta20

Name

Trachymela solitaria (Blackburn)

Reference specimen

2000-397, Wilsons Downfall, NE NSW

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 9.2-10.9 mm, ♂ 9.0-10.3 mm (7).

Colour:  head: generally uniformly dark-brown, infrequently clypeus is fractionally darker, rarely black, black-tipped mandibles; antennomeres 1-2 light-brown, sometimes with darker-brown or even black patches, 3-4 grading from light-brown to black, with distal parts usually darker, 5-11 black; pronotum: dark-brown, same shade as head, sometimes with a broad region bordering lateral margin (rarely front margin) of a lighter shade; scutellum: dark-brown, same or somewhat darker shade as adjacent region of pronotum, tending to black in specimens from southern Australia; elytra: dark-brown, same shade as pronotum, with thickened interstices of a fractionally lighter shade scattered over the disc, these often aligning in several longitudinal series, marginal area usually slightly lighter in shade, punctures often outlined in a fractionally darker shade, humeral callus concolorous with surrounds; venter: black, rarely with vaguely lighter-coloured patches on legs, epipleura variable, dark-brown or black.  Cuticular wax: usually a sparse layer of uniform light-brown shade over most of dorsal surface, with a prominently higher concentration on head and along lateral margins of pronotum and elytra.

Shape:  quite evenly rounded but relatively flat, highest point at or behind middle of elytral margin, ventrites project a considerable distance below elytral epipleura.  Head; punctures quite large (pd~2xef) and slightly unevenly but moderately densely distributed, sometimes partially obliterated by rugose interstices, rarely by relatively large unpunctured and raised spaces. Antennae long (AL ♂=47-52%BL, ♀=35-41%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; evenly curved transversely over discal region, lateral margins slightly explanate, sometimes very slightly, with foveal depression very shallow or even absent though always with a slight rounded elevation near its centre, puncturation always clearly impressed, evenly and quite densely distributed in discal area with a mix of puncture sizes usually present, the larger fractionally larger than those on head, becoming much coarser at lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin straight for a short distance then curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; smooth, unpunctured or lightly punctured with punctures somewhat smaller than those on adjacent area of pronotum.  Elytra; surface slightly flattened around highest point, punctures relatively evenly distributed, distinct only in area between humeral callus, summit and scutellum, elsewhere obscured by the network of thickened interstices, with much smaller punctures scattered very sparingly on the interstices, raised pimple-like interstices, usually lighter in colour than substrate, frequently align in longitudinal series, even joining to form thin, continuous ridges, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura slight (HCE/PW=0.27-0.29).  Venter; Prosternal process variable narrowest anteriorly or almost parallel-sided, open or lightly closed anteriorly, short setae present at rear of prosternal process and on mesosternum, a single seta (rarely 2-4) on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate; inner edge of posterior of epipleurae lacking setae.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=32-35%BL), in dorsal view, basal section considerably narrower than central section, sides slightly sinuate over central section, narrowing slightly from base before widening to reach its widest about 25% of length from apex, from there sides converge towards apex in a smooth arc, dorsal surface smoothly curved with faint dorso-median channel usually present at least for a short distance from ostium, dorsolateral channels well-defined, tip broad and blunt; in lateral view, almost evenly curved throughout most of length, then a pronounced downward bend shortly before apex, thinning towards apex over about 20% of length, tip strongly deflexed, flagellum thin, usually missing.

Similar species

 Ta166 - this species has a similar shape as Ta20, with greatest height either at or a little behind centre of elytral margin.  Its elytral surface is somewhat rougher, with small verrucae that extend some way above the level of the interstices, while Ta20’s elytral surface looks quite smooth.  Ta166 lacks the lighter-coloured longitudinal semi-continuous interstices that are present on most specimens of Ta20.

Notes

The identification as Trachymela solitaria Blackburn is by comparison with the type specimen in the BMNH.

Ta20 has been most frequently collected from Eucalyptus andrewsii, (Eucalyptus : Cineraceae) (10 specimens) and E. radiata (Eucalyptus : Eucalyptus) (5 specimens).
