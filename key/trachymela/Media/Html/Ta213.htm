Trachymela sp. Ta213

2020-045, Almaden, northern Queensland.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 7.5-8.0 mm (3), ♂ 7.74 mm (1).

Colour:  head: frons and clypeus dark reddish-brown, clypeal suture often narrowly dark-brown, mandibles reddish-brown with black tips, labrum yellowish-brown; antennae uniformly yellow-brown to light-brown; pronotum: reddish-brown, same shade as head, sometimes with vaguely defined fractionally darker patches around lateral and posterior edges of disc; scutellum: brown, same or fractionally lighter shade as adjacent area of pronotum, edged broadly in darker brown; elytra: reddish-brown, same or fractionally lighter shade than pronotum with diffuse areas of noticeably darker shade of brown often present in anterior half of lateral marginal area and adjacent to anterior edge, between scutellum and humeral callus, punctures noticeably darker than interstices, humeral callus same colour as surrounds; venter: head, thoracic ventrites and abdomen reddish-brown, sometimes almost uniformly so, or with some slightly darker areas on parts of thoracic ventrites, legs similar or fractionally paler, elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: head, disc of prothorax and most of elytra covered in a layer of brown or reddish-brown that is rather uneven, especially on elytra, where many rather thick clumps are scattered over the surface, with the intervening areas having a much thinner cover, the marginal area of prothorax and anterior half of elytral marginal area have a layer of white or light-grey wax, that colour is also present extending back from the anterior edge of elytra between scutellum and halfway to humeral callus, with the posterior edge of this area very uneven and a little in front of elytral summit.

Shape: rather narrow (width=64-66%BL), greatest height viewed laterally is a little in front of middle of elytral margin, thoracic ventrites extend below elytral margin.  Head; punctures relatively small (pd~1.5-2xef) and evenly and densely packed.  Antennae short (AL ♂=37%BL, ♀=32%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, slightly explanate at extreme margins, foveal depression absent or minimal with surface slightly but noticeably elevated in lateral part of foveal area, discal area smooth, puncturation very clearly impressed, mostly considerably larger than those on head and relatively evenly distributed and moderately dense, a scatter of much finer punctures among them, punctures becoming much coarser towards lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin straight for about 10% of length then curving inwards before straightening again to curve smoothly and very gradually towards rear angle.  Scutellum; smooth and unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over discal region, not explanate at margins, lightly curved longitudinally in anterior half, then curvature increasing considerably in posterior half, a barely perceptible depression extends across surface just in front of middle, punctures large and distinct, evenly distributed over almost all of surface, interstices quite convex over most of the elytral surface, this being most prominent in apical third where especially VR3 and VR5 form extended weals, elytral suture carinate in posterior third, elytral margin prominently sulcate in posterior third, margin sinuate, sometimes with anterior third oblique, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura small (HCE/PW=0.29-0.30).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided in anterior half or narrowing slightly anteriorly, open or lightly closed anteriorly, a light scatter of setae present along sides of prosternal process and on mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=30.4%BL), in dorsal view, sides sub-parallel from base to just behind ostium, then diverging fractionally before converging in a smooth curve towards apex, spatula small, tip small and rounded, dorsal surface smoothly curved with dorso-median channel faint but extending much of distance to basal sulcus, ostium relatively small; in lateral view, lightly and quite evenly curved from basal sulcus for about 80% of length, then bending downward prominently so that surface of spatula is almost perpendicular to long axis, relatively thick over central section, then thinning towards apex over 30% of length, apex deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta47 – an abundant species that overlaps the distribution of Ta213 and may be found on the same bloodwood host.  It differs from Ta213 by its smaller size, its greater width relative to body length (width=68-79%BL) and the quite different distribution of cuticular wax.

Notes

Ta213 is relatively easily recognizable from its very narrow shape and the characteristic pattern of cuticular wax in two colours.  It has been collected from the desert bloodwood, C. terminalis.
