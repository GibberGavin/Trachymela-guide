Adult Description

Length: ♀ ? mm, ♂ 7.25 mm (1).

[description based on the single (male) specimen available]

Colour:  head: uniformly mid reddish-brown, labrum dark brown; mandibles similar with black tips; antennae light brown, grading fractionally darker towards distal extremity; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with extensive black area that extends across almost the whole of disc except the anterior margin, the patch thins to occupy the anterior half of pronotum close to lateral margins of disc from where it extends towards the foveal area, broadening again before terminating in the centre of foveal area; scutellum: black; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with black markings present in 3 main features: the most prominent is a broad black band that starts at anterior margin between humeral callus and about half-way to margin and extends parallel to margin towards posterior, a second is a slightly diffuse patch situated on elytral disc just behind and above humeral callus, the third occupies most of the posterior half of elytral disc and is in the form of a number of thin black lines running both longitudinally and transversely to form an unevenly chequered surface of black and brown, a relatively thin black band also borders the scutellum and elytral suture anterior to summit; humeral callus black; venter: almost uniformly mid-brown, legs similar in shade, epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: a relatively thin sprinkling of light brown that becomes even less dense away from the lateral margins.

Shape: small, evenly rounded, greatest height viewed laterally clearly in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; punctures small to moderate in size (pd~1-2xef) and evenly and densely distributed.  Antennae short (AL ♂=39.2%BL, ♀=?), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, not noticeably explanate at extreme margins, lacking a foveal depression, an approximately round and very slightly elevated region is present in line with each eye, puncture sizes mixed in discal area, similar to head but density somewhat lower, puncturation clearly impressed in discal area, a little unevenly distributed and moderately dense (spacing ~2-3pd), becoming much coarser towards lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin very lightly curved for a short length and then curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; surface smooth, lightly punctured with punctures similar in size to the smallest on adjacent area of pronotum.  Elytra; surface evenly curved, punctures distinct and evenly and closely distributed (pd~2pd) over almost the whole surface, interstices flat for the most part, never obscuring punctures, with much smaller punctures scattered over them, interstices in posterior half only slightly more convex, surface continuous under humeral callus, elytral suture carinate in posterior third, lateral margins relatively straight with anterior third oblique.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.36).  Venter; Prosternal process narrowest anteriorly and gradually widening towards posterior, closed anteriorly, very sparse scatter of short setae at rear of prosternal process, mesosternum lacking setae, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; short (LPE=23.4%BL), in dorsal view, narrowest near basal sulcus and  parallel-sided for a short distance before sides diverge considerably to widest at about middle of ostium level, from where the sides converge towards apex very rapidly in a smooth arc, dorsal surface smoothly curved with well-defined, deep but short dorso-median channel, dorso-lateral channels broad very short, the oblique dorsal flaps extending for only a brief distance into ostium, ostium large and transversely oval, tip very short but broad, barely protruding; in lateral view, bent very slightly near basal sulcus, then ventral surface almost straight through to apex, rather thick, thinning towards apex over 25% of length, tip barely deflexed, flagellum thin and loosely curved.

Similar species

 Ta222 – this species occurs in the same area on the related Corymbia leichhardtii.  It is much smaller and lacks the large black patch on the pronotum as well as the checkered black and brown posterior half of elytral disc.

 Ta70 – a species that often has a similar checkered pattern on the elytra.  However, it is found on Angophora and has a distribution that is much more southerly.

Notes

The aedeagus of this species is rather similar in the shape of the ostium to species in the catenata group, though it does have a rather small and blunt, protruding tip rather than the thin spout-like apex of the catenata group. The only specimen was found on the unusual, broad-leaved yellow bloodwood, Corymbia peltata.
