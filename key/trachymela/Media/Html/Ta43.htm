Name

Trachymela sp. Ta43

Reference specimen

1998-0769a, Biloela, Central Queensland.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 7.3 mm (1), ♂ 5.7-6.9 (4) mm.

Colour:  head: uniformly mid-brown to reddish-brown, labrum similar or very somewhat paler, mandibles with dark-brown to black tips; antennomeres 1-4 uniformly straw-brown, 5-11 same shade or grading fractionally darker towards apex, the darker shade usually confined to distal region of antennomeres; pronotum: brown, very similar shade as head, sometimes with a small fuscous or black patches near lateral margins of disc, almost always with a small, round, black elevated area in centre of foveal area and a short oblique black streak between it and the posterior margin, sometimes these two are joined to make a “c”-shaped mark with the open end of the “c” facing the lateral margin; scutellum: brown, similar shade to pronotum, sometimes with fractionally darker lateral margins;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with small, round, raised black tubercles scattered unevenly over much of surface of disc except for the antero-median depression, many of these are present between humeral callus and scutellum where many coalesce to form longitudinal ridges, three longitudinally oriented bands of fully or partially coalesced black verrucae are present behind the antero-median depression: one starting about level with and just below elytral summit, the second near centre of discal area and starting a little posterior to first, the third starting a little towards margin and behind the second;  a disconnected line of black verrucae forms an interrupted transverse ridge along posterior margin of antero-median depression, and a line of black verrucae is situated along discal margin, intersecting with the transverse line behind depression, isolated black or dark-brown verrucae are present also along posterior third of elytral suture and often in posterior section of marginal sulcus; area around elytral summit black, humeral callus black; venter: mostly, including legs, medium brown with lateral sections of meta-thoracic ventrites much darker brown or even black, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Cuticular wax: somewhat uneven layer of a uniformly light brown colour, with a thin, transverse break in this layer extending across the middle of elytral disc, almost directly across summit, appearing as a fine, transverse line at first glance.

Shape: rounded, greatest height viewed laterally is clearly in front of middle of elytral margin (PGH ♂=51-52%BL, ♀=50%BL); Head; punctures quite large (pd~2-3xef), mostly evenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=35-39%BL, ♀=32.5%BL), filiform, outer segments noticeably flattened.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely with lateral margins only barely explanate, foveal depression absent, whole of foveal area slightly elevated above plane of curvature, always with a round, pimple-like. smooth and finely punctured elevation towards inside edge, puncturation clearly impressed, somewhat coarser than those on head and mostly quite evenly and densely distributed in discal area, usually interrupted by a thin, longitudinal unpunctured ridge in centre, punctures become much coarser from in marginal region, interstices smooth in discal area.  Thickened front margins smoothly join thinner lateral margins in a very smoothly angle somewhat greater than 90⁰, with lateral margin briefly straight before barely curving somewhat unevenly towards postero-lateral angle.  Scutellum; relatively smooth with a scatter of punctures similar in size to those on adjacent area of pronotum but less deeply impressed, sometimes very shallow.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over discal region, sometimes very slightly explanate at margins, evenly curved longitudinally from around summit to close to apex with curvature stronger anteriorly and posteriorly, a well-defined, shallow depression extends across surface just in front of middle, punctures moderately large and relatively evenly distributed between the verrucae and set deeply among the network of interstices sometimes with a very light scatter of fine punctures on interstices, small (black) verrucae, sometimes with a small puncture in centre, are scattered unevenly over much of elytral disc but prominently absent from anterio-median depression, with adjacent verrucae often joining into rough, raised ridges, their distribution is described in the colour section above, sometimes, especially in the scutellar region these are so closely packed that they form almost continuous raised black areas, verrucae along posterior edge of antero-median depression are usually slightly extended laterally and a short to more extended thin transverse ridge is often present, these combining to form a discontinuous, uneven transverse ridge, suture carinate in posterior third, surface continuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.39-0.40).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided over much of its length, open or very lightly closed anteriorly, short setae very sparsely distributed over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=29-34%BL), in dorsal view, sides slightly sinuate, narrowing very slightly from basal sulcus to about middle before broadening again towards ostial depression from where sides converge towards apex in a smooth, gradual and even arc, dorsal surface with well-defined dorso-median channel that terminates at posterior of ostial depression, dorsolateral channels well defined, two long ostial flaps protrude towards apex in the oval ostial depression, but terminate a little behind the small spatula; tip small and smoothly triangular; in lateral view, upper and lower surfaces almost evenly curved from basal sulcus to near apex, tip deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta90 – this species is quite similar in distribution of verrucae but lacks the black markings on the pronotum and the black common area around elytral summit.  Its aedeagus is very different.

 Ta104 – has similar black marking on the pronotum, but its humeral callus is not black and the black areas around the elytral summit are minimal.  Note that the aedeagus of Ta43 and Ta104 are quite similar in shape.

 Ta109 – another species that is of a similar size and looks quite similar at first glance.  However, the posterior half of the elytra contain verrucae that are discrete and it lacks the black marks on the pronotum.

 Ta112 – this species has similar black markings on the pronotum, but the dark verrucae in the posterior half of the elytra are isolated and discrete.

 Ta48 – as regards the external morphology and aedeagus, this species from North Queensland is very similar to Ta43 but is devoid of any black colouring dorsally.

Notes

Ta43 has so far been collected from just two widely separated locations in Queensland - near Biloela in the south and near the Lynd Junction in the far north.  At Biloela, it was found on Eucalyptus tereticornis and E. crebra, while the single specimen from the northern location was collected from an unidentified ironbark.

The northern specimen, [2020-049], is considerably smaller than the specimens collected from the southern location and it lacks black markings on the prothorax but is otherwise similar.
