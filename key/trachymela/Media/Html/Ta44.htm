Reference specimen

1998-0400, Dagg Falls, Killarney, S.E. Queensland

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 7.7-9.3 mm, ♂ 7.4-8.4 mm.

Colour:  head: mid- to dark- or reddish-brown, mandibles with black tips; antennomeres 1-4 straw-brown, 5-11 slightly or much darker brown, either all of similar shade or grading darker towards distal antennomeres, individual antennomeres unicolorous or with proximal extreme slightly paler; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with one or two black oval patches on each side of centre usually present, the larger one approximately in line with the inside edge of eye, a smaller one equidistant between it and the lateral margin is often absent, these patches may be indistinct or, rarely, completely absent; scutellum: completely dark-brown or black; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with a number of fractionally lighter-coloured longitudinal rows corresponding to raised interstices, between these lighter-coloured interstices fully or partially connect the longitudinal rows producing a chequerboard-like pattern of two slightly differing shades, rarely whole elytra is a single shade; punctures dark-brown, humeral callus concolorous with surrounds; venter: thoracic ventrites dark-brown, rarely almost black, abdominal ventrites fractionally paler, legs of slighly lighter shade, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown, rarely with posterior half partially black.  Cuticular wax: generally sparsely distributed, with dust more concentrated on lighter-coloured parts of elytral disc, enhancing the chequer-board pattern.

Head; punctures moderately and often quite unevenly sized (pd~2-3xef) and evenly to quite unevenly evenly and densely packed.  Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=42-48%BL, ♀=38-42%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression absent or very weak, a small round elevation usually present in line with centre of each eye, a very shallow but larger elevation on each side of discal area, puncturation usually clearly impressed, a mix of sizes present with the largest similar in size to those on head, slighly to quite unevenly distributed and moderately dense in discal area, becoming much coarser at lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; surface smooth or almost smooth, unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved, punctures distinct and relatively evenly distributed throughout but usually less distinct near apex, with much smaller punctures scattered sparsely on the interstices, verrucae absent or they are small and shallow, interstices often form longitudinal, slightly elevated ridges that are rendered more prominent by their lighter colouration, surface continuous, rarely slightly discontinuous, under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.32-0.34).  Venter; Prosternal process narrow, sometimes very narrow, anteriorly or, less often, almost parallel-sided, lightly closed anteriorly, short setae present sparsely over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; quite long (LPE=34-38%BL), in dorsal view, narrowing slightly and smoothly from base towards middle then broadening again to the widest point about 20% from apex before the sides converge towards apex in a smooth wide arc, dorsal surface smoothly curved with short dorso-median channel, dorsolateral channels converge at rear to form a large ostial region with ostial flaps extending far towards apex, tip protruding slightly, ostial opening large; in lateral view, almost evenly and quite strongly curved throughout its length, thinning towards apex over 15-20% of length, tip small and deflexed, protruding segment of flagellum is almost straight, very thin and whip-like.

Similar species

 Ta70 –specimens of Ta70 are on average slightly smaller than typical specimens of Ta44.  However, they can be almost impossible to distinguish, though the aedeagus is quite different.

Notes

This species is almost invariably (and commonly) found on Angophora sp.
