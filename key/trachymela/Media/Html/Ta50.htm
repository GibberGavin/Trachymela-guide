Adult Description

Length: ♀ ? mm, ♂ 7.35 mm.

[description based on the single (male) specimen available]

Colour:  head: dark reddish-brown, with fronto-clypeal suture and much of clypeus black, labrum yellowish-brown, mandibles mostly black, with small dark-brown areas behind tips; antennomere 1 dark-brown with both extremes paler, 2-3 light-brown, 4-11 dark-brown; pronotum: dark brown, similar shade as head, with two slightly irregular oval black marks on disc, one on each side of mid-line just anterior to centre, a prominent round black spot behind each eye and a small irregularly shaped black mark between it and posterior margin opposite humeral callus, lateral margins and anterior margins behind eyes black; scutellum: black;  elytra: brown, slightly lighter shade as pronotum, with many smooth, black, elevated pustules distributed irregularly over disc, the greatest concentration of these pustules is in a band that extends posteriorly from just behind humeral callus towards suture a little above apex, large black pustules are also present in area between scutellum, humeral callus and summit,  suture black in posterior half, a large black raised area is situated just outside of centre of edge of disc and 3 black pustules and black inner edge are present in posterior half of marginal sulcus, punctures fractionally darker than interstices, humeral callus black; venter: black, inside surface of elytral epipleura black.  Waxy dorsal covering: thin and uneven layer of greyish colour.

Shape:  rather wide (W/L=0.85) and quadrate viewed dorsally; viewed laterally, evenly rounded and greatest height just in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; moderately coarsely (pd~2xef) and quite evenly punctured. Antennae short (AL ♂=39.3%BL, ♀=?%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression evident only just behind anterior margin with a clearly defined and almost round elevated area just posterior to it, discal area slightly uneven with irregularly shaped raised regions on each side behind inner edge of eyes, puncturation on disc clearly impressed, fractionally larger than on head, quite evenly and relatively densely distributed with a sparse scatter of much finer punctures, becoming considerably coarser from level of eye to lateral margins, interstices quite smooth in discal area.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially almost straight initially then curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin, becoming noticeably thicker in posterior half.  Scutellum; smooth and unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely with slightly explanate margins, almost evenly and quite strongly curved throughout longitudinally, a thin short strip adjacent to elytral summit is smooth and very slightly elevated, punctures very large and distinct and relatively evenly distributed between the verrucae, verrucae are mostly discrete, circular or oval, prominently elevated and unpunctured, many are not aligned in longitudinal series though some such series are present mostly in posterior half, marginal sulcus prominent in posterior third, with 3 prominent verrucae and an elevated ridge along inner edge, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.35).  Venter; Prosternal process broad, parallel-sided, lighly closed anteriorly, setae scattered over prosternal process and mesosternum, lateral processes of mesosternum prominently wide and thick, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=26.9%BL), in dorsal view, almost parallel-sided from basal sulcus to about 25% of length from apex, then converging towards apex in a straight line so that spatula is roughly triangular and sharply pinted, dorsal surface lightly sclerotized with dorso-median channel very faint, dorsolateral channels long and well-defined; tip not clearly separated from rest of spatula, ostium large; in lateral view, moderately curved from base for a short distance, the curvature then reducing, thinning towards apex over 20% of length, tip slightly but clearly deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta83 – in general appearance, there is little to distinguish these species.  Ta83 is darker overall dorsally and it tends to have a line of verrucae that align along the rear of antero-median depression.  Ventrally, it can be distinguished by the prosternal process being expanded anteriorly and the thinner mesosternal lateral processes.

 Ta57 – this is a smaller species, its elytra appear much smoother, with less elevated verrucae and its antennae are shorter and its scutellum is usually punctured.  The epipleura of Ta57 are considerably further extended below the humeral callus than in Ta50 and the mesosternal lateral processes are not prominently thickened.

Notes

This species is very distinctive on account of the unusual shape of the lateral mesosternal processes.  It is also different from almost all species of Trachymela in its short, broad shape (viewed from above), though this specimen may not be typical of the species.  The specimen was caught off Eucalyptus persistens (Symphyomyrtus: Adnataria).
