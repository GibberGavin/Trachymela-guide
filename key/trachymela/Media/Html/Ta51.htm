Distribution

Name

Trachymela sp. Ta51

Reference specimen

2006-025, Ootann, Northern Queensland.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 6.9-7.3 mm (2), ♂ 6.0-6.6 mm (5).

Colour:  head dark reddish-brown, sometimes black along fronto-clypeal suture and pats or all of clypeus, labrum straw-brown, sometimes with dark infuscation; mandibles dark-brown with black tips; antennae uniformly light brown, or, rarely, distal antennomeres fractionally darker; pronotum: discal area very dark brown, a shade darker than head, or less often, almost black, marginal area usually a shade paler, small, smooth, round foveal elevation often black; scutellum: dark-brown or black, similar or fractionally darker than adjacent area of pronotum;  elytra: dark-brown, same or often a slightly lighter shade as pronotum, discal area with a scatter of black, smooth, shiny, elevated verrucae, mostly about 2-3 pd in size, mostly round or longitudinally extended ovals, a very few more extended ones form short, longitudinal ridges, center of discal margin with a more extended black raised area, posterior third of marginal sulcus often with 3 or 4 black verrucae, punctures similar in colour as interstices, humeral callus black or (less often) partially black; venter: head dark-brown with black gula, thoracic and abdominal ventrites mid- to dark-brown, prosternum sometimes slightly darker, legs similar or fractionally paler, inner portion of elytral epipleura black.  Cuticular wax: much of dorsal surface with a relatively uniform and moderately thick layer of brown wax, dotted with small patches of grey scattered irregularly along VR2, VR3, VR5 and VR10.  The extreme lateral margins of pronotum and elytra also have some greyish wax.

Shape: slightly flattened, greatest height viewed laterally around or just behind middle of elytral margin, dorsal surface with slight discontinuity in curvature between pronotum and elytra; Head; quite coarsely (pd~2-3xef) and a little unevenly punctured; antennae short (AL ♂=32-35%BL, ♀=30.6%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression absent with a clearly defined and almost round and puncture-free elevated area in centre of foveal area, a similar, less regular puncture free area is often present at lateral edges of disc, puncturation on disc clearly impressed, at least anteriorly, similar in size to those on head but better defined, often noticeably larger anteriorly, somewhat unevenly distributed with a scatter of much finer punctures on the interstices, interstices smooth and shiny, punctures becoming much coarser from level of inner edge of eye to lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; punctured, with punctures similar in size but shallower than on adjacent surface of pronotum, or unpunctured, often with surface texture rough.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over disc, then more steeply over marginal area, usually with a noticeably explanate elytral edge, lightly curved longitudinally in anterior 60%, after which curvature increases considerably, posterior angle close to 90°, antero-median depression very shallow, punctures moderately large and distinct and relatively evenly distributed between the verrucae, verrucae not clearly aligned in longitudinal series, individual verrucae mostly distinct, well elevated, rounded and smooth, unpunctured or, rarely, with one or two very fine punctures, sometimes several verrucae line up transversely just posterior to antero-median depression, posterior 25% of discal margin with an irregular line of verrucae with a large verruca situated between these and elytral suture, posterior third of marginal sulcus quite deep, often with a short row of pimple-like verrucae along its middle, posterior portion of elytral suture carinate, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.36-0.40).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided and relatively broad, open or (rarely) lightly closed anteriorly, setae scattered over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=27-28%BL), in dorsal view, widest near basal sulcus, then sides converging slightly to very slightly and quite evenly towards anterior over most of its length, converging suddenly becomes more rapid near front of ostium to form a relatively large and triangular spatula, dorsal surface lightly sclerotized with dorso-median channel very faint, dorsolateral channels prominent and quite long; tip protruding and almost triangular, ostium short, roughly oval, posterior shaped as a triangle with apex at centre, the dorsal flaps extend close to its apical edge when extended; in lateral view, lightly and quite evenly curved throughout, thinning towards apex over at least 30% of length, tip strongly deflexed, flagellum moderately thick.

Similar species

Ta57 – very closely resembles this species but tends to be slightly larger.  Its pattern of verrucae is very similar to that of Ta51 but specimens are usually very slightly darker both dorsally and ventrally.  The aedeagus is about 8% longer than in equivalent-sized specimens of Ta51, and tends to be almost parallel-sided, viewed dorsally, while that of Ta51 narrows very slightly from the basal sulcus towards apex.  These species also differ slightly in the arrangement of the cuticular wax.

Notes

All the specimens of this species were caught at M.V. light, so no host information is available.
