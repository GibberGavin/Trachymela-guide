Ta53

Name

Trachymela scabra (Chapuis)

Reference specimen

2002-357a, Deepwater, N.E. New South Wales

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 5.4(5.8)-8.2 mm [36], ♂ 4.8-6.5 mm [9].

Colour:  head: mid-brown to (less often) very dark brown, often with clypeus darker or black, black along centre line behind eys (often hidden by pronotum), labrum straw-brown, mandibles with dark-brown or black tips; antennomeres 1-4 uniformly straw-brown, 5-11 grading darker towards apex, sometimes black; pronotum: brown, similar shade or fractionally darker than head, anterior marginal areas sometimes a darker shade; scutellum: very dark brown or black, darker than, or rarely similar to, pronotum;  elytra: brown, same or slighly paler shade as pronotum, with scattered patches of darker brown or even black, these patches can vary from very small to quite large, the post-basal depression is usually darker than surrounding surface, sometimes the elytral lateral edge is margined in dark-brown or black, punctures same shade as interstices, humeral callus may be same colour or darker than surrounding area; venter: head and thoracic ventrites dark brown or black, abdominal sternites similar or slighly paler, legs similar or paler, inside surface of elytral epipleura black.  Cuticular wax: a light and relatively evenly to slightly unevenly distributed light-brown layer.

Shape: oblong, almost parallel-sided, for much of length viewed dorsally, greatest height viewed laterally is around or behind middle of elytral margin (PGH ♂~53-59%BL, ♀~54%BL); Head; punctures variable, sometimes moderately-sized and well-defined (pd~2-2.5xef), distribution somewhat uneven, or they may be extended longitudinally, or surface very rugose and punctures barely discernable. Antennae short (AL ♂=37-42%BL, ♀=32-40%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely with lateral margins only barely explanate, foveal depression absent or very slight, sometimes with a small round, pimple-like elevation towards inside edge, or whole of foveal area prominently domed above plane of curvature (northern specimens) discal surface rugulose (southern) or relatively smooth (northern specimens) puncturation clearly impressed or sometimes (southern) somewhat lost in surface rugulosity, coarser than those on head and mostly quite evenly and not very densely distributed in discal area, punctures become coarser in marginal region, interstices smooth or rough in discal area.  Thickened front margins smoothly join thinner lateral margins in a very smoothly rounded angle about or slightly greater than 90⁰, with lateral margin briefly straight before barely curving somewhat unevenly towards posterio-lateral angle; lateral edges often slighly crenulate or notched.  Scutellum; surface relatively smooth to rugulose, unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over discal region, then vertical or concave over marginal area, either evenly curved longitudinally or almost flat from just behind scutellum to about 2/3 of distance to apex with curvature increasing from there to apex, post-basal depression quite prominent but relatively compact, confined between VR3 and VR6, punctures moderately large and relatively evenly distributed between the verrucae and set deeply among the rows of interstices, many interstices in the form of prominent, long, unpunctured, elevated ridges with particularly VR2, VR3 and VR5 extending almost the full length of elytra, area below VR6 very rugulose, individual, discrete verrucae more evident, often partially connected longitudinally by much smaller ridges (that are sometimes black), with connections via transverse or oblique ridges to verrucae in different rows also present; suture carinate for most of its length, surface discontinuous under humeral callus; lateral margin slighly sinuous.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.39-0.36).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided over much of its length, lightly closed anteriorly, prosternal process and mesosternum devoid of long setae, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate, rarely notched; inner edge of posterior of epipleurae lacking setae.  Aedeagus; moderately long and very thin (LPE=34-35%BL), in dorsal view, sides almost parallel from basal sulcus to about middle of ostium, then narrowing very slightly to just before apex where the sides converge to form a broad, triangular apex, dorsal surface with well-defined dorso-median channel that terminates at posterior of ostial opening; in lateral view, bent slightly around basal sulcus, broadest just anterior to basal sulcus, ventral surface then relatively straight, svery slighly sinuous, to near apex, where it bends downwars noticeably, upper surface converges with lower towards apex, flagellum long and thin.

Similar species

Ta53 is an easily recognisable species from its almost rectangular shape (almost like a Cryptocephaline) and the long, continuous ridges on the elytra.

Notes

Identification as T. scabra (Chapuis) is by comparison with Chapuis’ description and Blackburn’s key items.

There are some differences in specimens from Queensland and those from New South Wales and Victoria.  The disc of the pronotum of northern specimens is smooth with well-defined and deeply impressed punctures, while that of the southern specimens is very rugose, with punctures not at all clearly defined.  The foveal area of the pronotum, if not depressed, is relatively flat in southern specimens, with at most a small verruca towards its inner edge, while that of northern specimens is elevated into a broad shallow dome.  Northern specimens tend to be smaller and darker than southern ones.  Queensland specimens (25 records) are almost invariably found on Eucalyptus crebra (Symphyomyrtus : Adnataria) while southern specimens have been collected almost exclusively from Maidenaria eucalypts, including E. acaciiformis (10), E. dalrympleana (1), E. nova-anglica (1) and unspecified Maidenaria (1).  These differences suggest that Ta53 may be composed of two separate species.
