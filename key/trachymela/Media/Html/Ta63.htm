Distribution

Ta63

Name

Trachymela sp. Ta63

Reference specimen

2004-677, Berridale, S.E. New South Wales

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 6.8-8.2 mm, ♂ 6.7-7.5 mm.

Colour:  head: mid-brown to dark-brown, outside edge of eye and usually a thin border around inside edge of eye, often extending to antennal insertions, black, fronto-clypeal suture sometimes fully or partially black, mandibles at least with black-tipped mandibles, sometimes more extensive black areas, labrum yellow-brown; antennomeres 1-3 light-brown, 4-11 grading towards a much darker shade of brown distally, rarely almost black at outer antennomeres; pronotum: brown, same shade or fractionally darker than head, very frequently with a black patch on each side of centre line and closer to anterior than posterior margin, often oval and obliquely placed with posterior closer to mid-line, sometimes the patch is smaller and more quadrate, a small, round black spot is sometimes present in middle of each foveal area; scutellum: black; elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with black, elevated moderate-sized verrucae scattered over much of surface but most heavily concentrated in a band just behind antero-median depression and in area between scutellum humeral callus and summit, an elongated thin, usually partially interrupted black ridge extends from anterior edge towards antero-median depression, elytral suture is usually thinly bordered in black for all or close to full length, punctures usually outlined in a fractionally darker shade than interstices, humeral callus black; venter: head black, prosternum black except at extreme lateral margin, thoracic ventrites very dark brown, sometimes grading to a slightly lighter shade towards margins, legs similar shade, abdominal ventrites usually fractionally lighter-coloured than thoracic, often with darker posterior margins, epipleura black.  Cuticular wax: relatively evenly-distributed light-brown to gray that leaves the verrucae prominently exposed.

Shape: somewhat flattened around elytral summit, greatest height viewed laterally is well in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; punctures moderately large (pd~2-2.5xef) and quite evenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=42-44%BL, ♀=29-34%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely over discal area, with foveal depression behind the outside edge either very feeble or absent, an small round elevated area in its centre, marginal areas continue curvature of discal area or are very slightly explanate, puncturation usually clearly impressed, similar in size or fractionally coarser than on head, quite evenly distributed and moderately dense in discal area, interstices sometimes very slightly elevated but never obscuring punctures, punctures becoming much coarser towards lateral margins.  Much finer punctures are scattered among the larger ones.  Front margins join slightly thinner lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin briefly straight before curving smoothly (sometimes a little unevenly) and gradually towards rear margin, becoming prominently thicker towards posterior.  Scutellum; very variable, surface smooth to quite rough, sometimes unpunctured, sometimes with shallow punctures slightly smaller than those on adjacent area of pronotum, rarely densely and very finely punctured.  Elytra; dorsal outline somewhat unevenly curved longitudinally, with section around elytral summit slightly flattened, a shallow transverse depression just anterior to middle, almost evenly curved transversely, margins not noticeably explanate, punctures distinct and relatively evenly distributed with much smaller punctures scattered very sparingly on the interstices, small to moderately large but prominent round verrucae, some with one or more very fine punctures, are present over much of disc though at slightly lower density over antero-median depression, a number of these tend to roughly line up behind antero-median depression into an interrupted transverse ridge that extends into marginal area, verrucal series often not clearly obvious though VR2 and VR3, particularly in posterior half, may be more prominently seriate, a prominent, usually longitudinally extended verrucae present near apex in VR3, interstices slightly rugulose, elytral suture carinate in posterior third, elytral margin slightly or moderately concave in posterior half, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.31-0.36).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided, closed anteriorly, a scatter of short setae present on prosternal process, absent from mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; relatively short (LPE=[25.5]27-29%BL), in dorsal view, slightly sinuous in central section, being broadest at base and about 75% of distance to apex, slightly thinner between these points, converging from broadest point towards apex in a smooth but concave arc, dorsal surface smoothly curved with dorso-median channel absent posterior to ostium, dorso-lateral channels well defined but short, tip protruding as a sharply pointed triangle, ostium large and round; in lateral view, very slightly curved for all of central section except near apex where curvature increases fractionally, quite thin and flat overall, tip slightly deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta45 - this species is of a similar shape and size and occurs in similar high-altitude locations as Ta63.  Ta63 lacks (or has very few) transverse wrinkles on the elytra and has much more robust verrucae.  It also has black colouring along the inside edge of eyes and the black patches on either side of midline of pronotum, which are absent in Ta45.

Notes

Ta63 has been found only on snow gum, Eucalyptus pauciflora.

Two specimens that are not from the Alpine Region differ from the typical Ta63 in several ways.  The aedeagus from a specimen from Victoria [2019-126], though being the same shape as that of other specimens, is significantly shorter.  The pronotum is completely devoid of black markings and the scutellum is dark brown rather than black.  No host data is provided, but E. pauciflora does not seem to have been recorded in the area where it was collected (Virtual Herbarium records).  This very likely represents a separate species.  The aedeagus of a specimen from south-east Queensland [2021-292], collected from Eucalyptus moluccana is more similar in shape (particularly in the sharpness of the apex) to the Victorian specimen.  It is similar in length to that of equivalent-sized alpine specimens.  A female from a nearby location [2021-336] also lacks black markings on the pronotum.
