Name

Trachymela sp. Ta66

Reference specimen

1998-0396c, Daggs Falls, Killarney, S.E. Queensland

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 5.2-6.3(6.7) mm [20], ♂ 4.7-5.9 mm [9].

Colour:  head: uniformly mid-brown, rarely some black near antennal insertion, mandibles with dark-brown to black tips; labrum slightly paler; antennomeres 1-6 light-brown, 7-11 usually similar, sometimes fractionally darker; pronotum: brown, same or (rarely) fractionally darker shade as head, some black-tinged areas may be present throughout, anterior margin of disc is sometimes tinged in black; scutellum: brown, same shade as adjacent region of prothorax, broadly bordered in dark-brown or black, rarely completely dark-brown or black;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with small, round, raised dark-brown or black wart-like protuberances (verrucae) scattered unevenly over much of surface of disc, greatest concentration of these usually just behind and in front of antero-median depression, often the dark colour extends a small distance around the verrucae and merges with neighbouring ones resulting in dark surfaces posterior to and anterior to antero-median depression , a narrow border surrounding the scutellum is often black, punctures are similar in colour to interstices, humeral callus black; venter: head black, thoracic ventrites dark-brown or black with legs of same shade, abdominal ventrites similar or very slightly lighter in shade, elytral epipleura with inner part of surface black, outer rim brown, less often completely brown.  Cuticular wax: somewhat uneven and sometimes thick layer of brown to orange-brown over much of the surface, with that in the anterior-median depression usually being paler (even grey) in colour and some paler longitudinal interrupted streaks often present in the posterior half of the elytra.

Shape: almost evenly rounded, greatest height viewed laterally is clearly in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; punctures fine to moderately sized (pd~1.5-2xef), evenly and densely packed. Antennae very short (AL ♂=37-39%BL, ♀=30-34%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely with lateral margins at most very slightly explanate, foveal depression absent or very shallow usually with a small and almost round elevated area in middle, puncturation clearly impressed, similar to or fractionally coarser than those on head and very evenly distributed in discal area, becoming much coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins, interstices usually smooth in discal area, rarely slightly rough but never so as to obscure punctures.  Front margins join lateral margins in a broad and smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially almost straight then curving smoothly (sometimes somewhat less so) and lightly to close to postero-lateral angle, where curvature usually increases sharply.  Scutellum; smooth and unpunctured, rarely with a few very shallow punctures.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over discal region, not noticeably explanate at margins, lightly curved longitudinally in anterior half, then very slightly more strongly curved, a very shallow depression extends across surface just in front of middle, punctures large and distinct and relatively evenly distributed between the verrucae and set deeply among the network of raised interstices, much finer punctures are scattered lightly among them, irregularly-sized but mostly quite small round verrucae, sometimes with a small puncture in centre, are scattered unevenly over elytral surface, usually these are isolated, but sometimes fine ridges run connect adjacent ones, elytral suture not carinate, marginal sulcus absent or very weak, surface continuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.37-0.39).  Venter; Prosternal process narrow anteriorly, widening evenly towards posterior, open or lightly closed anteriorly, short setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate; inner edge of posterior of epipleura lacking setae.  Aedeagus; short (LPE=27-33%BL), in dorsal view, sides parallel, at most very slightly sinuous, from base to ostial opening, then suddenly narrowing in a short concave arc before converging to tip in a straight or slightly sinuous line to form an extended, triangular spatula, dorsal surface membranous with a faint but broad dorso-median channel present, ostium small, tip triangular; in lateral view, bent at about 45˚ near base, lower edge towards apex from bend quite straight until close to apex where there is an almost imperceptible downwards bed, tip deflexed.

Similar species

Ta19 – this species is very similar in external morphology but is slighly larger (with only a small size overlap) and occurs mostly on monocalypt species.

Ta32 - this species is of a similar size, with small verrucae on the elytra.  It tends to be much lighter in colour ventrally, the head especially being brown rather than black, with the inside surface of the epipleura completely light brown and the elytral verrucae in a more regular arrangement.

Ta65 - another species of a similar size, with small verrucae on the elytra, but the verrucae tend to be of a more even size compared with those of Ta66 and are distributed more regularly, with interstices between them never black.  The inside surface of the epipleura of Ta65 are completely light brown.

Ta16 – this species has its verrucae more regularly distributed, has a black scutellum and completely black epipleura.

Notes

Ta66 is rather variable in its appearance, due to the differences in the amount of black on the elytra and venter in different specimens.  Typically, the elytra look very dark, with extensive black surface between the verrucae, while the inner epipleura are at least partially black.  In some specimens the black areas on the elytra are much reduced, with the extreme being no black on the elytra at all between the dark verrucae, while ventrally the epipleura may be completely brown and the ventrites dark brown rather than black.  This colour variation is present in both northern and southern specimens.

This widely distributed species has been collected mainly from box-barked eucalypts (likely Symphyomyrtus : Adnataria), including three records from E. melliodora, as well as records from E. blakelyi (1), E. camaldulensis (2) and E. prava (1).

A female specimen [1999-0695] included in this grouping from Lake St. Clair in NSW differs from typical Ta66 in having verrucae that are placed more clearly in longitudinal series and may belong elsewhere.
