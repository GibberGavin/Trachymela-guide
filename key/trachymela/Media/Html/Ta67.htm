Ta67

Name

Trachymela regularis (Blackburn)

Reference specimen

1999-0844b, Adaminaby, S.E. New South Wales

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 9.9-11.4 mm [14], ♂ (8.9)9.5-11.2 mm [15].

Colour:  head: mid-brown, usually with fronto-clypeal suture completely or partially black, black behind level of eyes (visible only when head extruded), mandibles black-tipped; antennomeres 1-3 light-brown, 8-11 very dark-brown or black, intermediate segments grade between these shades, often with distal extremes darker; pronotum: very variable, brown, same shade as head, usually with a large dark-brown or black patch on either side of centre, these patches may be irregular shaped, extending from near centre line just anterior of middle to edge of discal area and then as a broad band towards the posterior margin from there, sometimes they are much larger and occupy almost all of the disc, even joining across the centre forming a single large patch occupying all of discal area, rarely are these patches completely absent or very small; scutellum: brown, same shade as adjacent area of pronotum, usually broadly edged in a darker shade of brown, less often black; elytra: very variable, brown, usually same shade as marginal regions of pronotum, sometimes only marginal area is that shade while discal area is much darker brown, punctures often outlined in a slightly darker shade, longitudinal series of widely spaced small dark or black verrucae of approximately uniform size are often present, sometimes interstices between the verrucae are also dark-brown or black, forming longitudinal, interrupted dark or black ridges, in some specimens there is a broad black band extending from just behind and below humeral callus along elytral margin, humeral callus usually at least partly black, rarely concolorous with surrounding surface; venter: head brown, sometimes with black patches, pronotum black laterally, dark-brown in centre, meso- and meta-thoracic ventrites very dark brown, abdominal ventrites very-dark-brown, with 5th usually paler, legs mid-brown, epipleura brown.  Cuticular wax: a sparse to moderate layer of uniform shade that can be from light-grey to brown.

Head; punctures relatively large (pd~2xef) and quite evenly and densely packed, rarely with some rugose interstices. Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=47-49%BL, ♀=40-43%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; evenly curved transversely over disc, explanate towards margins with a broad shallow depression behind the outside edge of each eye, lacking a foveal peak, puncturation usually clearly impressed, the larger fractionally larger than those at rear of head, sometimes slightly obscured by rugose interstices, quite evenly to slightly unevenly and densely distributed in discal area, becoming considerably coarser at lateral margins.  Fine interstitial punctures are absent.  Front margins join lateral margins in a sharply rounded angle, less than 90⁰, sometimes almost mucronate, anterior half of lateral margin almost straight with posterior half then curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; unpunctured, rarely with a light scatter of very fine punctures.  Elytra; discal surface with a broad, transverse, shallow depression just in front of middle, fairly evenly curved over disc transversely but then quite prominently explanate at marginal area, punctures distinct and quite evenly distributed, the interstices forming a network of ridges that may include a few very inconspicuous transverse ones, lightly scattered with fine punctures, a few to many small pimple-like raised verrucae are always present and are arranged in longitudinal series, rarely these series include continuous ridges, surface discontinuous (depressed) under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.33-0.36).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided, closed anteriorly, short setae present on prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter (though often with several much shorter setae also present); front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate, tarsal pads in male large and roughly oval.  Aedeagus; large (LPE=40-45%BL), in dorsal view, almost parallel-sided for about 80% of distance from basal sulcus to apex, sometimes widening very slightly around ostium, sides then converge unevenly and slightly differently towards apex: left side curves inward and then outward again so that it is parallel on reaching apical edge which it joins in a smooth right angle, right side curves inward with convergence lessening slightly just before apical edge which it meets at slightly more than 90⁰, apical edge of spatula slightly oblique to centre line with left extreme further from basal sulcus, dorso-median channel very short past ostium but relatively deep, dorsolateral channels present and quite well defined, tip quadrate, with posterior edge roughly parallel to anterior edge, ostium oval and large, longer than broad; in lateral view, a noticeable bend near base at basal sulcus, then evenly and very slightly curved through most of length, thinning towards apex over 30% of length, tip deflexed.

Similar species

Ta68 – this species occurs in the same area and on the same host. It can be distinguished from Ta67 by its rounder pronotal front angles, the lack of black colouration on the disc of the pronotum and and its broader body shape generally.  In addition, Ta68 tends to have its verrucae less clearly aligned in longitudinal series than are those of Ta67.

Notes

Identification as Trachymela regularis Blackburn is from the description and comparison with the type specimen.  The asymmetric aedeagus makes it almost unique among known Trachymela – the only other species with this peculiarity is Ta128.

Ta67 is found over much of the highlands of southern New South Wales and Victoria and tends to be very abundant where it occurs.  The species has been collected exclusively from the snow gum, Eucalyptus pauciflora.
