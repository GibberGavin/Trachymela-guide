Name

Trachymela sublimbata (Chapuis)

Reference specimen

2004-436, Pinaroo, South Australia

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 10.0-10.6 mm, ♂ 8.7-10.4 mm.

Colour:  head: uniformly orange-brown with black-tipped mandibles; antennomeres 1-3 straw-brown, 4-11 similar or fractionally darker, outer 2-5 antennomeres usually with distal extremes dark-brown or black; pronotum: brown, same shade as head, with a quadrate black patch on each side in the foveal area, sometimes extending laterally to almost margin on one side and onto discal area on the other; scutellum: brown, same shade as pronotum, sometimes edged in a slightly darker shade; elytra: variable, brown, same shade as pronotum, with longitudinal bands of black or partially black colour which include (1) a broad band from just below humeral callus running along inside edge of disc and continuing the black area on pronotum - this band is usually complete to about 20% of distance to apex, but may become somewhat fragmented in its posterior section and be confined to a row of black verrucae, (2) a border to the elytral suture that invariable extends its full length but is usually thinner in the posterior half than near the scutellum, (3) a line of isolated small black verrucae extending from the anterior margin, halfway between scutellum and humeral callus and extending most of the way to apex, and (4) a similar line of verrucae between (1) and (3) that is often only partially black; (3) and (4) may rarely have black colouration between the verrucae; humeral callus dark-brown or black, punctures same shade as interstices; venter: completely orange-brown, sometimes with parts of ventrites, especially margins, slightly darker, legs similar shade, epipleura light-brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: uniformly light-brown and evenly distributed layer that can be quite dense but does not conceal the larger, black verrucae on elytra.

Shape: relatively flat and broad, elytral summit clearly in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; punctures moderately large (pd~2-2.5xef) and quite evenly and densely packed, a puncture-free area sometimes present along mid-line behind eye level. Antennae short (AL ♂=39-44%BL, ♀=36-39%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely over discal area, with a well-defined, longitudinal and relatively narrow depression situated behind the outside edge of each eye, marginal areas continue curvature of discal area or are almost imperceptibly explanate, puncturation usually clearly impressed, rarely more vague, similar in size or fractionally finer than on head, very evenly distributed and dense in discal area, interstices in discal area relatively smooth, never obscuring punctures, punctures becoming much coarser from foveal depression to margin.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin straight for some distance before curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; surface slightly rough, unpunctured.  Elytra; surface noticeably unevenly curved in lateral view, with relatively strong curvature from scutellum to a little past summit where curvature reduces somewhat towards apex, transversely, evenly curved over disc with clearly explanate margins, punctures usually very poorly defined, sometimes almost obsolete, small, round verrucae, some with a very fine central puncture, are present in 3-4 longitudinal series over disc, other, even smaller verrucae, are scattered between these rows, interstices quite smooth with a few transverse wrinkles usually present only near middle of discal margin, surface discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura large (HCE/PW=0.39-0.41).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel-sided before widening towards posterior, open or lightly closed anteriorly, a scatter of short setae present on prosternal process, absent from mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; very large (LPE=40-47%BL), in dorsal view, initially sub-parallel from base to near middle of central section, then broadening slightly towards widest point opposite ostium and close to apex, converging from there towards apex in a smooth arc, dorsal surface smoothly with well-defined and very long dorso-median channel present, dorso-lateral channels well defined but short, tip protruding in a smoothly rounded triangle, ostium oval, ostial flaps extending far forward; in lateral view, almost straight for all of central section except near apex, then curving suddenly downwards, thinning rapidly over the downturned section, tip deflexed.

Notes

Identification of this species as Trachymela sublimbata is by comparison with the original description of Chapuis and a specimen labelled as such (including the aedeagus) in the BMNH.  As this is a quite distinctive and large species there is little doubt about the identification.

Ta74 occurs in much of the mallee areas of southern Australia, from Western Australia to New South Wales and has been collected from a variety of species, including Eucalyptus dumosa and E. incrassata.
