Reference specimen

2007-374, Mitchell Plateau, N.W. Western Australia

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 6.6-7.8 mm [6], ♂ 6.0-6.6 mm [2];

Colour:  head: uniformly dark reddish-brown to almost black, with black-tipped mandibles, labrum yellowish-brown; antennomeres 1-2 light-brown, 4-11 either similar or grading slightly (rarely much) darker towards apex; pronotum: uniformly reddish-brown to almost black, same shade as head, sometimes darker along extreme lateral margins; scutellum: same colour as adjacent area of pronotum, usually edged with a slighly darker shade; elytra: reddish brown to almost black, same shade as pronotum, often uniformly so, sometimes with diffuse areas of slightly darker shade of brown scattered throughout, punctures similar in shade to interstices, verrucae and longitudinal ridges sometimes fractionally paler than surrounding surface, humeral callus same colour as surrounds; venter: prosternum and thoracic ventrites dark reddish-brown or black, same shade as dorsal surface, edges of ventrites sometimes slighly darker, legs, abdominal ventrites similar to thoracic or rarely slightly lighter in shade, elytral epipleura brown.  Cuticular wax: brown and rather thick over much of dorsal surface.

Head; punctures moderately sized (pd~1.5-2xef) and quite evenly and densely packed. Antennae short (AL ♂=37-41%BL, ♀=31-32%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, minimally explanate at extreme margins, foveal depression absent with a broad, round, slighly elevated area in the same place, discal area smooth, puncturation usually clearly impressed but may be more vaguely impressed partially, mostly similar-sized to those on head and evenly and moderately densely distributed in discal area, punctures becoming much coarser at lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin gently convex for close to half its length then, at widest point of pronotum, becoming almost straight.  Scutellum; surface smooth or slightly rough, unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over discal region, not explanate at margins or slighly explanate only very close to margin, lightly curved longitudinally in anterior half, then slightly more strongly curved, a barely perceptible depression extends across surface just in front of middle, punctures large and distinct, evenly distributed over almost all of surface, similar in size on margin to disc, interstices convex scattered lighly with very fine punctures, mostly in the form of longitudinal weals that are particularly prominent in apical third, a few isolated verrucae between these weals, surface continuous or slightly discontinuous under humeral callus, lateral margin sinuate.  Vertical extension of epipleura small (HCE/PW=0.27-0.31).  Venter; Prosternal process moderately wide, narrowing very slightly anteriorly, open anteriorly, setae lacking or very sparse along prosternal process, a very few short ones on mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate; inner edge of posterior of epipleura lacking setae.  Aedeagus; moderately-sized (LPE=34-37%BL), in dorsal view, sides sub-parallel from base to around rear of ostium before converging towards apex, with the angle of convergence increasing slighly near apex to form an extended and broad triangular spatula, dorsal surface smoothly curved with faint and short dorso-median channel, tip clearly protruding, ostium moderate in size; in lateral view, ventral surface quite strongly curved from just anterior to basal sulcus for about ¾ of length, where it straightens out, thinning gradually and evenly from about mid-point to prominent and clearly recurved spatula, flagellum relatively thick at exit from ostium where it bends sharply twice before tapering down in diameter.

Notes

Individuals of this species have been collected from bloodwood (Corymbia s. lat.) hosts, with the only species-level record being from C. latifolia.
