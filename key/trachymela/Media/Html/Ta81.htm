Distribution

Ta81

Name

Trachymela sp. Ta81

Reference specimen

1999-0857, Nimmitabel, S.E. New South Wales.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 6.9-7.2 mm (4), ♂ ? mm (0).

Colour:  head: frons and clypeus mid-brown, labrum yellow-brown, mandibles brown with black tips; antennomeres 1-5 uniformly light brown, 6-11 usually similar, sometimes grading towards a darker shade, but never black; pronotum: brown, slightly darker shade as head, sometimes with vague patches of very slightly darker brown; scutellum: brown, same shade as adjacent area of pronotum, usually edged in a darker brown at sides;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, sometimes completely so, but more often with small, darker brown or even black raised tubercles scattered quite sparsely and unevenly over much of surface of disc, their density lower in antero-median depression, a few tubercles rarely aligned into longitudinal series, a few just behind antero-median suture may line up very loosely into a transverse row, punctures similar or slightly darker than interstices, humeral callus concolorous with surrounds; venter: almost uniformly mid-brown, including legs, with only edges of thoracic ventrites sometimes slightly darker, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: a very light dusting of light-brown.

Shape: almost evenly rounded longitudinally over most of elytral surface, with outline disjunct at intersection of pronotum and elytra, relatively flat (H/L= ♀=44-46%BL), greatest height a little in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; punctures moderately sized (pd~2-2.5xef), evenly and densely packed. Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=?%BL, ♀=35-37%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely with lateral margins barely explanate, foveal depression very shallow or absent, an almost round slightly elevated area situated behind each eye, puncturation clearly impressed, slightly more coarse than on head and usually very evenly distributed in discal area, becoming noticeably coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins with surface between punctures often more coarsely textured near margins, interstices a little uneven in discal area but never obscuring punctures with small elevated areas usually present on each side of centre.  Thickened front margins join the thinner lateral margins at an angle slightly less than 90°, sometimes producing a faintly mucronate antero-lateral angle, lateral margin then curving very gradually towards rear margin, so that prothorax is widest very close to rear margin.  Scutellum; smooth or finely wrinkled, unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over discal region, a very shallow depression extends across surface just in front of middle, surface appears reticulated due to the prominent raised interstices running longitudinally and laterally, punctures set deep among these but quite evenly distributed over the surface, small verrucae are scattered unevenly over much of surface although with a lower density in anterio-median depression, the verrucae are roughly round, often with quite irregular outlines,  sometimes very inconspicuous (if not darker than surrounding surface), sometimes with a very fine puncture in centre, those just posterior to antero-median depression may form a very rough transverse series; surface continuous or slightly discontinuous under humeral callus, lateral margin of elytra prominently sinuous; vertical extension of epipleura small (HCE/PW=0.29-0.30).  Venter; Prosternal process narrowest anteriorly then widening gradually, lightly closed anteriorly, setae sparsely distributed over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single long seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; unknown.

Similar species

 Ta106 – this species differs from Ta81 by being a little larger, having an almost completely black ventral surface (including the epipleura), and a wider (HCE > 0.3) and non-sinuate lateral elytral margin.

Notes

No males of this species were available for examination.  Ta81 has been recorded from Eucalyptus viminalis.
