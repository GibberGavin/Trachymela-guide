Adult Description

Length: ♀ 5.6-6.0 mm (4), ♂ 4.7-5.2 mm (6).

Colour:  head: dark to very dark-brown, rarely completely black, clypeus black, labrum yellowish-brown, mandibles dark-brown with black tips; antennomere 1 light to mid-brown, 2-3 light-brown, 4-11 somewhat to considerably darker, rarely almost black; pronotum: dark brown or black, similar shade as head, if dark-brown, then often with several small round black patches present on disc: one directly behind inside edge of each eye and one behind outside edge of each eye are most frequently present, less often, another pair are situated between the first patches and midline; scutellum: black;  elytra: dark-brown or black, same shade as pronotum, if not black then sometimes with diffuse, irregular, often large black patches, the most prominent of which are usually centred on elytral summit and another extending posteriorly from humeral callus, punctures are similar in colour as interstices, humeral callus black; venter: black, inside surface of elytral epipleura black.  Waxy dorsal covering: moderate to thick patches of mostly greyish dust interspersed with brown, the scutellum and adjacent regions on pronotum and elytral suture are dust-free or very sparsely dusted, and there is a band of sparsely distributed dust running from anterior edge of elytron opposite humeral callus posteriorly to meet suture about ¾ of distance to apex. 

Shape: slightly flattened and relatively broad, greatest height viewed laterally is clearly in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; moderately coarsely (pd~2-2.5xef) and evenly to slightly unevenly punctured. Antennae very short (AL ♂=34-35%BL, ♀=31-33%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression absent or very indistinct usually with an almost round and often puncture-free elevated area in middle, discal area smooth in centre but sometimes with small raised, less densely punctured regions on each side behind inner edge of eyes, puncturation on disc clearly impressed, puncture size slightly larger than on head, quite evenly and not relatively densely distributed with interspersed much finer punctures, becoming much coarser from level of inner edge of eye to lateral margins, interstices smooth in discal area.  Thick front margins join thinner lateral margins in a very smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin curving smoothly or slightly unevenly and gradually towards rear margin.  Scutellum; smooth and usually unpunctured, less often sparsely and finely punctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over disc, slightly to considerably explanate from disc to margin, gently convex longitudinally from scutellum to summit, then curvature increases from summit to apex, a antero-median depression very shallow, punctures very large and distinct almost all the way to apex and relatively evenly distributed, verrucae absent although interstices may be thickened and fractionally elevated in a few places, posterior third of marginal area variable, often moderately concave but sometimes almost flat, surface continuous or very slightly discontinuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.35-0.39).  Venter; Prosternal process variable, most commonly broad, and parallel-sided, open anteriorly, but sometimes narrowest anteriorly and lightly-closed (type specimen), a sparse scatter of setae over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=25-27%BL), in dorsal view, parallel-sided, broad (WPE = 0.29-0.32*LPE) from basal section to close to apex from where sides converge smoothly towards apex, tip small and triangular, dorsal surface smooth, dorso-median channel absent, ostial flaps extend forward almost to apical extreme of ostium, ostium round; in lateral view, ventral surface moderately and smoothly curved about 1/2 of length from base, then curvature reduces smoothly but considerably until apex, tip deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta62 – once divested of the dorsal dust covering, these two species of similar size have a very similar appearance with their lack of verrucae and evenly distributed and large punctures on the elytra.  Ta62 has brown internal epipleural surfaces, while those of Ta82 are black.

Notes

Ta82 has been collected only from the ironbark species Eucalyptus crebra and E. melanophloia.  It has a similar pattern of grey dust interspersed with flecks of brown on its dorsal surfaces as Ta83 and Ta145, but is much smaller and less ruggedly sculptured than these species.
