Name

Trachymela sp. Ta90

Reference specimen

2017-384, Wilson’s Downfall, N.E. New South Wales

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 5.7-7.25 mm, ♂ 6.2-6.8 mm;

Colour:  head: uniformly mid-brown to dark-brown, sometimes with darker infuscations around rear of eyes, rear of head black (visible only if considerably extended), mandibles with dark-brown to black tips; labrum similar or very slightly paler, infrequently slightly darker; antennomeres 1-4 uniformly straw-brown, 5-11 same shade or grading slightly darker towards apex, the darker shade often confined to distal region of antennomeres; pronotum: almost uniformly brown, very similar shade as head, sometimes with vague darker patches, rarely with a small, round, black elevated area behind each eye; scutellum: brown, similar shade to pronotum, with broad, darker brown border, sometimes fully dark brown;  elytra: brown, same shade as pronotum, with small, round, raised black tubercles scattered unevenly over much of surface of disc except for the antero-median depression, their density tends to be highest between humeral callus and scutellum and just behind antero-median depression, adjacent tubercles are sometimes joined by black, raised interstices, rarely this produces a patchwork of brown and black over much of disc, punctures are similar in shade to interstices, humeral callus black; venter: meso- and meta-thoracic ventrites and legs wholly black or (rarely) very dark-brown, with some small slightly lighter-coloured patches present mainly on legs, inside surface of elytral epipleura brown.  Waxy dorsal covering: variable, may be relatively evenly distributed, moderately thick layer of a uniform, slightly paler brown shade than surface of insect, or less thick, more uneven, with dust concentrated in antero-median depression of elytra.  The dark verrucae are visible above the dust layer.

 Shape: rounded, greatest height viewed laterally is clearly in front of middle of elytral margin (PGH ♂=51-52%BL, ♀=50-52%BL); Head; punctures moderately sized (pd~2xef), evenly and densely packed. Antennae very short (AL ♂=41-45%BL, ♀=31-33%BL), filiform, outer segments noticeably flattened.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely with lateral margins very slightly explanate, foveal depression absent to moderately deep, often with a clearly defined and almost round elevated area near inside edge, puncturation clearly impressed, similar to or very slightly more coarse than those on head and rather evenly distributed in discal area, becoming much coarser from foveal depression to lateral margins, interstices smooth to slightly rough in discal area, never so rough as to obscure punctures.  Thickened front margins smoothly join thinner lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin almost straight or barely curving for 25-75% of length towards rear margin, then curving more strongly towards posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; smooth and unpunctured.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely over discal region, sometimes slightly explanate at margins, lightly curved longitudinally in anterior half, then slightly more strongly curved, a well-defined, shallow depression extends across surface just in front of middle, punctures quite large and relatively evenly distributed between the verrucae and set deeply among the network of raised interstices, smaller punctures are scattered among them, small, mostly round verrucae, sometimes with a small puncture in centre, are scattered unevenly over much of elytral disc except for anterio-median depression, raised interstices form lateral and longitudinal ridges which connect some of the verrucae, verrucae in the scutellar angle usually align into short, longitudinal rows, sometimes joined into longitudinal ridges, surface continuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura considerable (HCE/PW=0.37-0.39).  Venter; Prosternal process parallel-sided over most of its length or narrower anteriorly, lightly closed anteriorly, short setae very sparsely distributed over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta on anterior and median trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; very long, needle-like (LPE=37-41%BL), broadest at junction with basal section, from where the sides are initially sub-parallel or converging very slightly until about 30% of length from apex, then converging slightly more rapidly towards apex, dorsal surface smoothly curved with short, faint dorso-median channel, tip very slightly protruding, ostium small, dorsal flaps protruding far forwards; in lateral view, bent at junction of basal and central section, then almost straight almost to apex, thinning near apex, tip slightly deflexed.

Notes

On first glance, individuals of this small species often look somewhat like Ta23, with the dust frequently concentrated in the elytral depression and along the elytral edges.

Ta90 has been found almost exclusively on the smooth-barked group of eucalypts that include Eucalyptus deanei.
