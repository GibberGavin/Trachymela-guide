Adult Description

Length: ♀ ? mm (0), ♂ 5.0-5.7 mm (2).

Colour:  head: frons dark-reddish brown, clypeus black, with the black sometimes extending a little past fronto-clypeal suture, labrum black, mandibles completely black; antennomeres 1-2 light-brown, 3-11 grading darker towards outer segments, sometimes outer segments black; pronotum: mostly glossy dark reddish-brown, fractionally darker than head, anterior margin between eyes broadly black, the black coolour sometimes extending halfway towards posterior edge on disc, a small, round black area sometimes present in centre of foveal depression;  scutellum: black; elytra: mostly glossy black, with some slightly paler, dark reddish-brown patches mostly along lateral margin and anterior margin, humeral callus black; venter: black, with some slighter paler areas present towards lateral extremes of meso- and meta-thoracic ventrites and abdomen, inside surface of elytral epipleura black.  Waxy dorsal covering: unknown.

Head; punctures moderately large (pd~1.5-2xef), slightly unevenly distributed, longitudinal interstitial ridges sometimes obscuring punctures.  Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=37-38%BL, ♀=?%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal only apparent in anterior half of pronotum, where it is relatively well defined, a prominent, slightly elevated round pustule which is less densely punctured than surrounding area situated behind centre of eye midway between anterior and posterior edges, with the pronotal surface elevated noticeably in surrounding area, discal area with surface relatively smooth and shiny, punctures similar in size to those on head but sometimes quite shallow, usually more deeply impressed along rear margin, evenly to slightly unevenly and moderately densely to more sparsely distributed (spacing~2-3pd), becoming much coarser towards lateral margins.  Front margins join lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially almost straight or curving almost imperceptibly and smoothly towards rear margin with the curvature becoming greater in posterior third, broadest about 2/3 of distance to posterio-lateral angle.  Scutellum; smooth, punctured with punctures similar or slightly finer than on adjacent surface of pronotum, but usually less deeply impressed.  Elytra; surface evenly curved, punctures surrounded by convex interstices, closely spaced, clearly impressed and quite evenly distributed except around the flat elytral summit where they are very fine, interstices slightly thickened in places to form small or moderate sized, very slightly elevated areas, with small, round and more prominently elevated verrucae scattered among these, some with very fine central punctures, but with no clear arrangement in series, posterior third of elytral suture carinate, posterior section of marginal area very weakly concave, surface continuous  or rarely very slightly discontinuous under humeral callus. Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.36-0.37).  Venter; Prosternal process almost parallel or narrowing very slightly anteriorly, lightly closed or open anteriorly, prosternal process and mesosternum with a scatter of long setae, a single long seta present on anterior, median and posterior trochanters; front edge of metasternum beveled, broadly rounded.  Aedeagus; moderately long (LPE=33-35%BL), lanceolate, in dorsal view, widest near basal sulcus with sides initially converging uniformly and very gradually to just behind level of ostium, from where rate of convergence increases fractionally, dorsal surface membranous with a very short dorso-median channel present, surface posterior to ostium with a shallow concavity, ostium in shape of a long oval with dorsal flaps nor quite extending as far as spatula, spatula relatively long, tip triangular; in lateral view, bent at about 150˚ a short distance anterior to basal sulcus, lower edge towards apex from bend quite straight until close to apex, dorsal surface initially parallel to ventral surface until about 20% of distance from apex then converging gradually towards it, tip deflexed.

Similar species

 Ta92 – this is a superficially similar and similarly-sized species that occurs over the same area as Ta93.  It differs in the texture of the elytra – whereas Ta93 has clearly elevated areas and verrucae, those of Ta92 are barely elevated at all, with much of the surface seeming smooth and flat and “elevated” areas in all but posterior third of elytra appearing more as black areas on an otherwise flat surface.  The antennae of Ta92 are usually almost uniformly light- to mid-brown in colour, never grading to black at the distal end.

 Ta94 – a species that is remarkably similar in appearance.  Individuals are slightly larger than those of Ta93, tend to be a lighter shade of brown and lack its glossy sheen and neither the clypeus nor inner epipleura are black.  The aedeagus of the two species is essentially identical in structure but that of Ta94 is about 10% shorter as a proportion of body length.  Since only two specimens each are currently available for these species, there is a possibility that they are conspecific, albeit with an unusually large size range.

Notes

This is a very dark and glossy species, a member of the triangulum group.  Both (male) specimens were caught at M.V. light, so nothing is known of host preferences of this species and whether females are larger (as is common in this group).
