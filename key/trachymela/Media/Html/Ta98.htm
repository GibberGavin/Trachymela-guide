Distribution

Ta98

Name

Trachymela inaequalis (Blackburn)

Reference specimen

2004-401, Ngarkat National Park, South Australia.

Adult Description

Length: ♀ 7.8 mm (1), ♂ 6.7-7.2 mm (4).

Colour:  head: either completely black or (less often) frons dark reddish-brown with black clypeus, labrum yellowish-brown, mandibles black; antennomeres 1-4 light or mid-brown, 4-11 much darker, usually black; pronotum: completely black, rarely with a slightly lighter, dark-reddish brown tinge in foveal area; scutellum: black;  elytra: disc either completely black, or, less commonly, with a dark reddish-brown longitudinal band in area between scutellum and humeral callus, marginal area usually dark reddish-brown, less often black, punctures are similar in colour as interstices, humeral callus black; venter: black, including legs, inside surface of elytral epipleura black.  Waxy dorsal covering: a rather sparse sprinkling of brown or grey, slightly denser over anterior portion of pronotum and head.

Shape: quite evenly rounded over most of length, with curvature increasing suddenly close to apex, so that elytral suture meets ventral surface at about 90⁰, greatest height viewed laterally is about or barely in front of middle of elytral margin; Head; quite coarsely (pd~2-3xef) and somewhat unevenly punctured. Antennae moderately long (AL ♂=42-44%BL, ♀=37.1%BL), filiform.  Pronotum; almost evenly curved transversely, foveal depression very shallow with a small, clearly defined and almost round elevated area in middle, discal area smooth but slightly uneven, punctures on disc clearly impressed, puncture size variable, punctures similar in size to those on head, but more even-sized and more regularly distributed, becoming much coarser from level of inner edge of eye to lateral margins, interstices mostly smooth in discal area.  Front margins join thinner lateral margins in a smoothly rounded right-angle, with lateral margin initially straight for about half of length, then curving smoothly and gradually towards rear margin, becoming noticeably thicker in posterior half.  Scutellum; smooth with a scatter of punctures that are shallow and considerably smaller than on adjacent surface of pronotum.  Elytra; surface evenly curved transversely with margins slightly explanate, antero-median depression absent or barely discernable, punctures small but distinct and relatively evenly distributed between the verrucae, verrucae are moderately large (2-3 pd) discrete, circular or slightly oval, prominently elevated, some with fine punctures in centre, and very evenly distributed across the elytral disc, almost all are included within 7 or 8 longitudinal series, two or three verrucae just posterior to antero-median depression sometimes partially joined by transverse ridges, posterior third of marginal area prominently concave, usually with a line of small verrucae along it, posterior third of elytral suture carinate, surface continuous under humeral callus.  Vertical extension of epipleura moderate (HCE/PW=0.36-0.38).  Venter; Prosternal process parallel-sided, sometimes quite broad, closed anteriorly, setae scattered over prosternal process and mesosternum, a single seta (rarely 2-3) on anterior and median trochanter, 1 long seta usually present on posterior trochanter; front edge of metasternum beveled, truncate.  Aedeagus; long (LPE=34-36%BL), in dorsal view, sides almost parallel from basal sulcus to middle of ostium, usually diverging very slightly near middle of ostium so that its maximum width is at that point; from middle of ostium towards apex, sides converge very gradually and evenly at first, the convergence increasing suddenly to form a discontinuity in the otherwise smoothly curved sides, sides tend to be much darker than most of dorsal surface, dorsal surface smoothly curved with a usually short and faint dorso-median channel, dorso-lateral channels well-defined but short, ostium small, dorsal flaps terminate a little before spatula,  tip small; in lateral view, lightly curved just past basal sulcus then lower surface almost straight to apex, thickness decreasing evenly toward apex from about middle of ostium, tip deflexed.

Similar species

This completely black species with its regularly arranged, prominent and round verrucae is unlikely to be mistaken for any other species.

Notes

The name has been determined by comparison with a photo of the type specimen of Paropsis inaequalis (Blackburn, 1896), located in the BMNH, its original description and type locality.  The measured size of the type specimen falls within the range of males of Ta98, and its A-A/BL ratio corresponds to that of a similar-sized specimen of Ta98.

Ta98 has been recorded from the stringybark Eucalyptus arenacea (Eucalyptus : Capillulus) and an unidentified tree with box-type bark.
