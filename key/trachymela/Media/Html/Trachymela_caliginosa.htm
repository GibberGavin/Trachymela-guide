Original description

[Original description shown in figure, translated from Latin using ChatGPT]

Oval, reddish-chestnut and pitchy.  Pronotum sparsely and unevenly punctured, with larger punctures toward the sides, there broadly depressed and bearing a blunt tubercle; reddish-chestnut with the tubercle and a diffuse median spot blackish-pitchy.  Elytra deeply punctate-striated, reddish-chestnut with pitchy markings; interstices convex and tuberculate, the tubercles unequal, rounded or oblong.  Underside blackish-pitchy. 

Length 8 mm.

Notes

Blackburn (1896) deals with T. caliginosa as part of his subgroup ii (i.e., those species without "strongly marked characters", whose greatest height is not or scarcely in front of the middle of the elytral margin and whose elytra are depressed under the humeral callus).  Within that group, he characterises it as:

(1) inner edge of humeral callus distinctly nearer to lateral margin of elytra than to suture;

(2) sides of prothorax more or less explanate;

(3) elytra not having well-defined continuous costae;

(4) puncturation of the elytra not particularly fine;

(5) upper surface of elytra in general, or at least the verrucae, black or nearly so;

(6) explanate margins of prothorax relatively narrow (less than about 1/3 of width of discal part);

(7) median verrucae of prothorax scarcely defined;

(8) prothorax not with contrasting dark in the middle and pallid sides;

(9) elytral verrucae large, all isolated, nowhere confused with interstitial rugulosity;

(10) puncturation of prothorax not asperate;

(11) puncturation of prothorax sparse, coarse and irregular.

This is almost certainly a member of the pustulosa-group of species, and is possibly Ta234, a similar-sized species that has a distribution of verrucae on the elytra that closely resembles that of T. caliginosa.
