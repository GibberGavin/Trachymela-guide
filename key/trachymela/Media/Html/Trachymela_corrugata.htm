Trachymela corrugata

Name

Trachymela corrugata (Chapuis, 1877)

Type specimen

Not examined.

Original description

[Original description shown in figure, translated from Latin using ChatGPT, 04/2026]

Shortly ovate, shiny, reddish-brown; pronotum densely, finely, somewhat irregularly punctate, at the sides depressed, punctate-rugose, with blunt tubercles; elytra finely punctate-striate, on the dorsum obscurely depressed, toward the middle distinctly transversely rugulose, the interspaces almost all, behind the base and toward the apex, granulose-tuberculate; underside of the body yellowish-ferruginous, variegated with pitchy.  Length 7 mm.  Sydney.

Notes

Blackburn (1896, p. 640) includes corrugata in the list of species that he considered insufficiently described, and not to have any very salient characteristics, and so were not included in his key.  He clearly did not see any examples of this species.  Weise (1923) considered corrugata to be the senior synonym of P. transversalis (Blackburn).  It is not clear whether he saw types of either of these species.  Chapuis' description of corrugata certainly does not emphasize the presence of the central transverse weal that is such a prominent feature in transversalis, stating only that it is "versus medium distincte transversim rugulosis" (towards the middle distinctly transversely rugulose).

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 96).

Weise, J. 1917, "Uber australische Chrysomelinen", Archiv für Naturgeschichte, vol. 82, 124-141 (page 133).
