Trachymela exsul

Name

Trachymela exsul (Blackburn, 1896)

Type specimen

Location: BMNH

Label data: “Type” (red-bordered circle), “Blackburn coll. 1910-236.”, “Paropsis exsul Blackb.”, “6164 N.S.W. T N.QD.” [on card with insect, the last phrase seemingly crossed out] 

Male; Size: 8.2 x 6.3 x 3.7 mm; A-A: 1.42 mm.  A uniformly dark-brown species, verrucae basically absent or small and inconspicuous; scutellum with a few shallow but relatively large punctures.

Original description

[Original description shown in figure, translated from Latin using ChatGPT, 03/2026]

(Male) Broadly ovate, fairly convex, the greatest height (seen from the side) placed about the middle of the elytra; fairly shining; pitchy, variegated with reddish (especially almost entirely on the head, on the sides of the prothorax, on the margins of the elytra and some indistinct spots, at the base of the antennae, and on the sides of the abdomen); head rather closely and roughly punctulate; prothorax longer than broad in the ratio nearly 2⅔ to 1, widened from the apex to the middle, behind the apex very faintly transversely impressed, rather closely and somewhat finely (at the sides fairly coarsely) punctate, sides fairly arcuate, distinctly rather narrowly flattened, posterior angles obtuse; scutellum finely punctulate; elytra distinctly depressed beneath the humeral callus, behind the base somewhat roundly impressed, rather closely and fairly finely subseriately (at the sides a little less closely, posteriorly a little more finely) punctate, with some verrucae scarcely distinct, arranged somewhat in rows; interstices slightly rugulose (more distinctly toward the apex); marginal area moderately broad, well separated from the disc (by a groove broadly interrupted before the middle and rather deep behind the middle); humeral callus a little more distant from the suture than from the lateral margin; basal ventral segment sparsely somewhat strongly punctate.  Length 3⅘ lines (~8.5 mm), width 3 lines (~6.75 mm).

Notes

Blackburn (1896) deals with P. exsul as part of his subgroup ii (i.e., those species without "strongly marked characters", whose greatest height is not or scarcely in front of the middle of the elytral margin and whose elytra are depressed under the humeral callus).  Within that group, he characterises it as:

(1) inner edge of humeral callus distinctly nearer to lateral margin of elytra than to suture;

(2) sides of prothorax more or less explanate;

(3) elytra not having well-defined continuous costae;

(4) puncturation of the elytra decidedly fine (Blackburn makes a note that in P. exsul, is not very much finer than those species in the other pair of the couplet);

(5) prothorax not much narrowed in front, widest at middle.

 

References

Blackburn, T. 1896, "Revision of the genus Paropsis. Part I", Proceedings of the Linnean Society of New South Wales, vol. 21, no. 4, 637-693 (page 665).
