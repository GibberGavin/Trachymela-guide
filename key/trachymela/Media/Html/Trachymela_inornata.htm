Paropsis inornata, description

Trachymela inornata

Name

Trachymela inornata (Blackburn, 1896)

Type specimen

Not examined.

Original description

[Original description shown in figure, translated from Latin using ChatGPT, 04/2026] 

♂. Allied to P. alta; less broad, much less convex; pitchy, with the antennae reddish toward the base; pronotum from the apex a little less long broadened beyond the middle; scutellum smooth; elytra behind the base scarcely at all transversely impressed; third antennal segment not longer than the fourth; the rest as in P. alta.  Length 4 lines (~8.5 mm); width 3½ lines (~7.4 mm).

Female. Somewhat more convex than the male.

Notes

Blackburn (1896), deals with T. inornata as part of his 'subgroup iii' (i.e., those species without "strongly marked characters", whose greatest height is not or scarcely in front of the middle of the elytral margin and whose elytra are not depressed under the humeral callus).  Within that group, he characterises it as:

(1) no postbasal impression on disc of elytra;

(2) elytral verrucae concolorous with or darker than general surface;

(3) puncturation of prothorax more or less close and at most moderately strong;

(4) seriate arrangement of elytral verrucae and especially the punctures scarcely evident;

(5) elytra exceptionally finely punctulate;

(6) form notably less wide [than P. alta]; elytra longer than wide.

 The main differences that Blackburn gives in his description between this species and T. alta is the less convex form, the smooth rather than punctured scutellum and the 3rd antennal segment not longer than the fourth.  It is likely an alta-group species, some of whose members are within 'subgroup ii'.

References

Blackburn, T. 1896, "Revision of the genus Paropsis. Part I", Proceedings of the Linnean Society of New South Wales, vol. 21, no. 4, 637-693 (page 688).
