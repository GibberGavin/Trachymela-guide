Trachymela orbicularis

Name

Trachymela orbicularis (Chapuis, 1877)

Diagnosis and Notes

In his revision of the genus Paropsis (Blackburn, 1896), he places this species into his "Group iv", a mixed bag of species characterized by (1) the sides of the prothorax evenly arched (not mucronate in front), (2) the puncturation of the elytra in about 20 rows, and (3) the elytra devoid of verrucae.   This group includes just a few species of non-verrucose Trachymela - the bulk of its species are now placed in Peltoschema or Paropsisterna.  In his key to the species in this group, orbicularis is identified by:

AA. Subbasal impression of elytra wanting or almost wanting;

B. Elytral edging of scutellum convex and ridge-like;

C. The spots on the prothorax run at equal intervals transversely across the middle line.

This is very likely Ta70. Another species (Ta44) has a similar arrangement colours on the interstices (light-brown, interrupted by dark-brown).  However, Chapuis gives the length of the type as 7 mm and its type locality is Sydney, both of which point towards Ta70 as the more likely species.

Type specimen

Location: RBINS (not examined)

Paratype in BMNH, label data: “Paratype” (yellow-bordered circle), “Coll. Chapuis”, “Brit. Mus. 1953-297”, “TYPE”, “Type”, “det Trachymela orbicularis Chap.”

This is a species with a fairly smooth dorsal surface, brown with darker patches in an almost chequerboard fashion.

Original description

[Original description shown in figure 3, translated from Latin using ChatGPT, 03/2026]

Somewhat rounded, reddish-chestnut; head densely punctured, with the vertex marked with black; pronotum strongly but rather sparsely punctured, distinctly depressed at the sides and strongly punctate-rugose; with four black transverse spots; elytra deeply punctate-striated; with five narrow reddish-chestnut interstices, interrupted by dark brown, the four broader interstices dark brown, marked with reddish spots; underside of the body dark pitchy; legs ferruginous.  Length 7 mm.  Sydney, New South Wales

References

Blackburn, T. 1897, "Revision of the genus Paropsis. Part II", Proceedings of the Linnean Society of New South Wales, vol. 22, 166-189 (page 182).

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 90).
