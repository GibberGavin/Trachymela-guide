Paropsis papuligera, description

Trachymela papuligera

Name

Trachymela papuligera (Stål, 1860)

Type specimen

Not examined.

Notes

Blackburn (1896) places this species into his Subgroup i, these being mostly not very closely-related species that exhibit "strongly marked characters".  The strongly marked character for this species is "The humeral calli elevated into large ear-like processes".  Ta04 is clearly this species, and was identified as such by B. J. Selman (pers. comm, 1979).

References

Blackburn, T. 1896, "Revision of the genus Paropsis. Part I", Proceedings of the Linnean Society of New South Wales, vol. 21, no. 4, 637-693 (page 644).

Stål, C. 1860, "Till kannedomen om Chrysomelidae", Öfversigt af Kongelige Vetenskaps-Akademiens Förhandlingar, Stockholm, vol. 17, 455-470 (page 465).
