Trachymela rustica

Name

Trachymela adelaidae (Blackburn, 1896)

Type specimen

Location: BMNH

Label data: “Type” (red-bordered circle), “6154 For. Reefs T”, “Blackburn coll. 1910-236.”, “Paropsis rustica Blackb.”

Female; Size: 10.15 x 7.8 x 5.1 mm; A-A: 1.55 mm; HCE: 2.26 mm (obtained by measurement from lateral view in photo).

Head brown; pronotum brown, surface noticeably wrinkled; scutellum almost black, smooth; elytral surface brown, noticeable anterior-median excavation; many black verrucae, often small, many with a central puncture, many in the middle extended a little in a lateral direction, those above humeral callus are in in short longitudinal rows; excavate under the humeral callus.

Notes

This relatively large species does not seem to be any of the coded species (Ta01-238), and it is likely I have not seen it.  Blackburn indicates its type locality as Forest Reefs, in central NSW, not far from the town of Orange.
