Trachymela rustica

Name

Trachymela adelaidae (Blackburn, 1896)

Type specimen

Location: BMNH

Label data: “Type” (red-bordered circle), “6154 For. Reefs T”, “Blackburn coll. 1910-236.”, “Paropsis rustica Blackb.”

Female; Size: 10.15 x 7.8 x 5.1 mm; A-A: 1.55 mm; HCE: 2.26 mm (obtained by measurement from lateral view in photo).

Head brown; pronotum brown, surface noticeably wrinkled; scutellum almost black, smooth; elytral surface brown, noticeable anterior-median excavation; many black verrucae, often small, many with a central puncture, many in the middle extended a little in a lateral direction, those above humeral callus are in in short longitudinal rows; excavate under the humeral callus.

Original description

[Original description shown in figure, translated from Latin using ChatGPT]

Oval, rather narrow; moderately convex, the greatest height (seen from the side) situated far before the middle of the elytral margin; rather dull; reddish-brown (with the elytra bearing numerous verrucae arranged fairly evenly but not regularly in rows, and with several indistinct streaks, the sterna and epipleura black; antennae darkened towards the apex).

Head and prothorax nearly as in P. laeviventris, but the latter more transverse (about 2 3/4 to 1), the sides scarcely flattened and less strongly rounded.

Humeral depression of the elytra, the submarginal groove (this less abruptly interrupted in the middle), the subbasal impression, and the epipleura as in P. Chapuisi.

Elytra rather strongly and fairly closely punctured in somewhat serial arrangement; the interstices on the disc scarcely rugulose (if the verrucae are disregarded), the marginal area rather coarsely rugulose.

Basal ventral segment finely punctured.

Length 5 lines, width almost 4 lines.

Notes

This relatively large species does not seem to be any of the coded species (Ta01-238), and it is likely I have not seen it.  Blackburn indicates its type locality as Forest Reefs, in central NSW, not far from the town of Orange.
