Trachymela scabra

Name

Trachymela scabra (Chapuis, 1877)

Type specimen

Location: RBINS

Label data: “Australie”, “Coll. Chapuis”, “TYPE”, “P. scabra Chp”, “Type”, “Trachymela scabra (Chap) ref. M. Daccordi 2003”

Original description

[Original description shown in figure, translated from Latin using ChatGPT]

Oblong, somewhat truncate, somewhat convex, dark reddish-chestnut; head rugose-punctate, vertex blackish; pronotum deeply variolose-punctate and somewhat obliquely bi-impressed, laterally coarsely rugose-punctate; elytra deeply punctate-striated, the interstices striolate, the interstices somewhat raised toward the apical third; reddish-chestnut with black variegations, with a rather deep, somewhat quadrate, black discal impression; underside body dark pitchy-red. Length 7 mm.

Notes

Blackburn (1896) deals with T. scabra as part of his subgroup i (i.e., those species with "strongly marked characters").  Within that group, he characterises it as:

(1) pronotum "normal" (i.e, sulcate down the middle and not unusually wide);

(2) a well-defined antemedian discal excavation on the elytra;

(3) form oblong, very little convex.

The is no doubt that this is Ta53, the characteristic oblong form and longitudinal ridges on the elytra making it easy to identify.

References

Blackburn, T. 1896, "Revision of the genus Paropsis. Part I", Proceedings of the Linnean Society of New South Wales, vol. 21, no. 4, 637-693 (page 645).

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 91).
