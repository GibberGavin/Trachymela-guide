cancellata

Name

Paropsis cancellata Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

This name is a synonym of T. exarata (Chapuis), and thus the same as Ta09.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn (1896, p.640) indicates that he has not seen this species and does not include it in his key to the species.

From the description and examination of the type specimen, it is clear that this species is the same as T. exarata, (and thus Ta09), a widely distributed species in north-eastern Australia on Corymbia.  The species is quite variable over its range, and specimens, particularly from the southern part of the distribution, often have the verrucae more prominent and dark, as in this type.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 5) by ChatGPT, 04/2026:

Ovate, convex, reddish-brown; pronotum rather sparsely and fairly strongly punctate, with small punctures intermixed, at the sides depressed and with larger punctures somewhat confluent, with a median black patch extending from the base to the apex; elytra somewhat dull, punctate-striate, with small and sparse punctures, the interspaces with nine (rows of?) tubercles (the first, near the scutellum, obsolete); three spots, a sublateral stripe, and the tubercles are black; underside of the body and legs ferruginous.  Length 9 mm.  Brisbane.

Similar species

This is very much like a paler version of T. asperula (Chapuis), with the rows of verrucae on the elytra slightly darker than most of the rest of the elytral surface.  This is almost certainly a specimen of Ta09, showing the slight differences in colouring of southern specimens of that species.

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 95).
