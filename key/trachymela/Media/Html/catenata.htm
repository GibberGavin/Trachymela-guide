catenata

Name

Paropsis catenata Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

The holotype of catenata was examined in May 2008 and is clearly a specimen of Ta01.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn (1897) deals with P. catenata as part of his 'subgroup iv' (i.e., those species without "strongly marked characters", and whose greatest height is considerably in front of the middle of the elytral margin).  Within that group, he characterises it as:

A. Prothorax distinctly explanate at the sides;

B. Subhumeral depression present;

C. Elytral verrucae concolorous with derm, closely set in regular series and not large;

DD. Marginal part of the elytra scarcely distinct from the discal part.

Type specimen

Location: RBINS

Label data: /“Coll. Chapuis”/ "TYPE"/ “P. catenata Chp K.G.S. Melb”/ “Trachymela catenata (Chap) ref. M. Daccordi 2003”/.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 4) by ChatGPT, 03/2026:

Shortly ovate, reddish-brown.  Head finely punctulate, with a median black spot, somewhat triangular.  Pronotum sparsely and finely punctate; laterally impressed and with larger punctures crowded; the disc on each side bearing a black longitudinal stripe extending from base to apex.  Elytra moderately punctate-striate; interspaces very finely punctulate; with nine interstices obtusely tuberculate, marked with a lateral stripe and three black spots.  Length 8 mm.   Sydney, Melbourne, Nouvelle Galles du Sud [New South Wales]

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 93).
