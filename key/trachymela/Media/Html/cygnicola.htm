Name

Paropsis cygnicola Blackburn, 1897

Corresponds to

 This does not seem to correspond to any of the current species codes.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn (1897) deals with P. cygnicola as part of his 'subgroup iv' (i.e., those species without "strongly marked characters", whose greatest height is considerably in front of the middle of the elytral margin).  Within that group, he characterises it as:

AA. Prothorax not (or scarcely) explanate at sides;

BB. The verrucae unpunctured or nearly so;

C. Elytra not having a sharply defined discal transverse wheal-like ridge;

DD. Subhumeral depression wanting;

E. Elytral interstices but little rugulose, at least not to the extent of obscuring the punctures and verrucae;

F. Elytra (at least on hinder part) studded with sharply defined isolated bead-like verrucae;

G. The elytral verrucae small;

H. The elytral puncturation fairly strong and not particularly close;

II. The post-basal impression of elytra very strong and from a certain point of view defined behind by a slight ridge.

This is likely a species in the group related that includes Ta06 (scattered verrucae on on elytra, particularly near apex and behind anterior-median groove, which is well-defined); however it is likely that I have not yet seen a specimen.

Not examined.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 1) by ChatGPT, 04/2026:

♀. Broadly somewhat oval, very convex, with the greatest height (seen from the side) situated before the middle of the elytral margin; moderately shiny; testaceous-ferruginous, with the sterna (in part), tarsi (in part), the extreme apex of the antennae, and the verrucae (wart-like elevations) of the elytra somewhat pitchy.  Head densely and rather finely punctulate.  Prothorax broader than long in the ratio of about 2⅔:1, slightly widened a little beyond the middle from the apex, transversely impressed behind the apex, punctulate like the head (coarsely rugulose at the sides); sides rather strongly rounded, not flattened; posterior angles absent.  Scutellum shiny, almost smooth.  Elytra not depressed below the humeral callus, but strongly transversely impressed behind the base; rather strongly and somewhat in rows punctured (more so at the sides, less strongly behind); with fairly numerous, not very small verrucae (absent in the impressed basal part), scarcely arranged in rows; interstices scarcely rugulose (more distinctly so behind), but here and there appearing as transverse wrinkles under certain lighting; marginal area fairly broad, rather distinctly separated from the disc by a somewhat obsolete but fairly continuous groove, directed outward obliquely and moderately strongly; inner margin of the humeral callus not more distant from the suture than from the lateral margin of the elytra.  Basal ventral segment closely but less strongly punctured.  Length 3 (scarcely) lines [~6.3 mm]; width 2⅖ lines [~5.1 mm].

References

Blackburn, T. 1897, "Revision of the genus Paropsis. Part II", Proceedings of the Linnean Society of New South Wales, vol. 22, 166-189 (page 174).
