explanata

Name

Paropsis explanata Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

Ta151.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn did not see the type specimen of explanata, but includes it in his key (Blackburn, 1896).  He deals with this species as part of his 'subgroup ii' (i.e., those species without "strongly marked characters", whose greatest height is not or scarcely in front of the middle of the elytral margin and whose elytra are depressed under the humeral callus).  Within that group, he characterises it as:

A. Inner edge of humeral callus distinctly nearer to lateral margin of elytra than to suture;

B. Sides of prothorax more or less explanate;

C. Elytra not having well-defined continuous costae;

D. Puncturation of the elytra not particularly fine;

E. Upper surface of elytra in general, or at least the verrucae, black or nearly so;

F. Explanate margins of prothorax wide (each about 1/3 of width of discal part);

G. Postbasal impression of elytral disc feeble;

H. Prothorax at its widest notably behind the middle;

I. Elytral puncturation (or at least its seriation) much obscured, especially behind, by close rugulosity of the interstices.

This is Ta151.  The identification is relatively certain, this being the largest species in the genus and apparently restricted to S.W. Western Australia.  The closely related and morphologically very similar Ta73 seems to be restricted to S.E. Australia.

Type specimen

Not examined.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 1) by ChatGPT, 04/2026:

Oblong-ovate, somewhat convex; blackish-pitchy; pronotum rather densely and strongly punctate, broadly depressed at the sides, with pitting of a variolose kind; elytra deeply punctate-striate, broadened posteriorly, the interspaces equally and densely punctate; some interstices slightly raised, shortened, toward the apex nodulose or tuberculate; on the disc transversely rugulose; lateral margin very broadly expanded, somewhat reflexed.  Length 15 mm.  Swan River.

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 94).
