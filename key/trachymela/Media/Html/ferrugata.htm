ferrugata

Name

Paropsis ferrugata Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

This could be Ta210, see Diagnosis and Notes below.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn (1896) deals with T. ferrugata as part of his subgroup ii (i.e., those species without "strongly marked characters", whose greatest height is not or scarcely in front of the middle of the elytral margin and whose elytra are depressed under the humeral callus).  Within that group, he characterises it as:

A. Inner edge of humeral callus distinctly nearer to lateral margin of elytra than to suture;

B. Sides of prothorax more or less explanate;

CC. Elytra with well defined continuous costae.

This is almost certainly the same as a species that I believe to be a variant of Ta210 [eg, GM-082].  The sculpture of the elytra are almost identical, with verrucae most pronounced in several rows in the apical region.  The aedeagus is of the same form, and the length and the HCE/BL ratio of the type specimen are in the right range.  If the equivalence is confirmed, T. acclivis (Blackburn) would be a junior synonym of T. ferrugata.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 7) by ChatGPT, 03/2026:

Oblong-oval, reddish-chestnut.  Head densely punctate-rugose, the middle of the vertex and the front each with a black spot.  Pronotum slightly uneven, densely and strongly punctured, rugose toward the sides and slightly depressed.  Elytra deeply and rather regularly punctate-striated; interstices slightly raised, the third interstice (especially toward the apex and laterally) somewhat tuberculate.  Underside testaceous and variably reddish.  Length 9–10 mm.  Tasmania.

References

Blackburn, T. 1896, "Revision of the genus Paropsis. Part I", Proceedings of the Linnean Society of New South Wales, vol. 21, no. 4, 637-693 (page 655).

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 92).
