Fig. 1. Paropsis fusconotata, description

fusconotata

Name

Paropsis fusconotata Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

This is very possibly the same as Ta163.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn does not deal with this species at all, indicating that he has not seen it.  The description is very brief and inadequate, but does indicate the presence of a number of series of dark verrucae on the elytra and a black suture.  One likely candidate for this species is Ta163, which is of the same size and occurs in the same area as the type locality, has the black series of verrucae, often a black elytral suture, its scutellum is punctate, and the pronotum is as described by Chapuis.

Type specimen

Not examined.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 1) by ChatGPT, 03/2026:

Oblong-ovate, convex, reddish-brown; pronotum rather densely and finely punctate, at the sides with some larger punctures, ornamented with a depression and a blunt black tubercle; scutellum punctulate; elytra moderately punctate-striate, with the suture and with tubercles or verrucae arranged more or less in longitudinal series, black.  Length 5½ mm.  King George Sound

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 94).
