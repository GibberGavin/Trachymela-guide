A

acervate:

Distributed in clusters.

aedeagus:

The male reproductive organ

Aedeagus, lateral view (Narrandera, N.S.W., 2001-089a)

antero-median depression:

A transverse depression of the elytral surface situated just anterior to its centre.  The depression extends from just below the elytral suture a little in front of the summit towards the margin of the disc.  In some species, it is more pronounced due to the presence of verrucae or even a continuous ridge along its posterior margin.

C

cuticular wax secretion:

Small globules of wax, secreting from glands in the cuticle over most of dorsal surface (and sometimes ventral surface) of the adult (wax bloom).  These waxes start to appear soon after adult ecdysis and seem to be produced over most of the beetle's life.  The wax layer resembles a coating of small grains of dust (sometimes mixed with short, threadlike strands) on the surface of the cuticle and may be a single colour or several colours and shades.  The colour of the wax and its pattern is usually characteristic for a species.  The wax is easily rubbed off during handling and most museum specimens retain little, if any, particularly if the insect has been stored in alcohol.

E

elytra:

The anterior, chitinous wings that serve as the coverings for the hind wings.  At rest, these meet in a straight line down the middle termed the elytral suture.

epipleura:

Epipleura (singular: epipleuron) are the deflexed or inflexed portions of the elytra, immediately beneath the edge (de la Torre-Bueno, J.R, 1985).  In Trachymela, the sides of the elytra are extended vertically, concealing the epipleura which face inwards.  In Trachymela, the inner surface of the epipleura may be brown, black or partially black.

H

humeral callus:

The humeral callus (pl: humeral calli) is a more or less rounded tuberculate elevation of the elytral surface situated just above and behind the humerus.

humerus:

The basal exterior angle of the elytra.

I

interstices:

The space between punctures.

L

labrum:

The flat, smooth structure that covers the mandibles from above, forming the roof of the mouth.

lanceolate:

Lance or spear-shaped; oblong and tapering towards the apex.

S

scutellum:

The scutellum is a triangular piece that is part of the dorsal surface of the mesothorax situated between the bases of the elytra and the pronotum.

V

verrucae:

Verrucae (singular: verruca) are small to medium-sized wart-like elevations on the surface of the elytra.  They are often darker than the integument, but may be of a similar colour.  Frequently, they have a small puncture at their centre.  The size, arrangement and colour of the verrucae is an important character in the identification of Trachymela species.
