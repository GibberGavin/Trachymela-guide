Trachymela ignorata Weise, 1923

Diagnosis and Notes

Weise (1923) notes that Paropsis chapuisi Blackburn, 1986 is a pre-occupied name (Paropsis chapuisi Duvivier 1884, now Paropsides chapuisi) and, in a footnote in his paper, proposes ignorata as nom. nov.
