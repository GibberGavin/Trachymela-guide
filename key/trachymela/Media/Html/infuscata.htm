infuscata

Name

Paropsis infuscata Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

No known species can be definitively associated with this description.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn does not deal at all with this species, indicating that he has not seen it.  The description is rather inadequate.  Chapuis mentions 4 small black vittae on each elytron, though says nothing about their positioning.  It is just possible that this is Ta133, but that is little more than speculation.

Type specimen

Not examined.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 1) by ChatGPT, 04/2026:

Somewhat rounded, bright ferruginous.  Pronotum rather sparsely and irregularly punctate, the interspaces finely punctulate; toward the sides with broad, rather shallow, crowded punctures, scarcely depressed and furnished with a broad, blunt tubercle, and adorned with two black spots.  Elytra brown, dull, moderately punctate-striate, everywhere very finely punctate-granulate; the alternate intervals finely tuberculate, and the elytra with four more or less distinct black vittae.  Length 8 mm.  Australia.

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 93).
