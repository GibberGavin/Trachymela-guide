multiseriata

Name

Paropsis multiseriata Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

Chapuis' description does not indicate the presence of any verrucae, so (assuming it is a Trachymela) it is likely to be the same as Ta77.  The four black spots, placed transversly on the pronotum, the dark scutellum, and the elytral interstices being irregularly marked with dark spots all agree with the typical colouring of Ta77.  The length indicated, as well as the occurence near Sydney also fit that species well.  It is therefore a synonym of T. orbicularis (Chapuis).

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn (1897, p. 181) notes that it is not clear whether multiseriata belongs to his Group iii or Group v.  I have not been able to find where he deals with it in his revision if he does so.

Type specimen

Not examined.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 1) by ChatGPT, 04/2026:

Ovate, convex, testaceous; head densely punctured, reddish-testaceous, vertex black; pronotum rather densely and strongly punctured, laterally somewhat depressed and punctate-subrugose, reddish-testaceous with four black spots placed transversely; scutellum dark fuscous; elytra rather deeply punctate-striate, the punctures dark, the interstices punctulate, testaceous, irregularly marked with dark spots; underside of body dark fuscous; legs ferruginous.  Length 7–8 mm.  Sydney

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 90).
