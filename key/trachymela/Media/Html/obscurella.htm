Paropsis obscurella, description.

obscurella

Name

Paropsis obscurella Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

No known correspondence with any of the species codes.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn (1897, p. 181) notes that he has not seen either the type or any specimens of this species, and does not include it in his description.

It is difficult to determine from the original description whether this is a Trachymela species - it seems to lack verrucae, so may be an orbicularis-group species.

Type specimen

Not examined.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 1) by ChatGPT, 03/2026:

Obovate, convex, reddish-chestnut; pronotum densely and strongly punctured, laterally somewhat impressed and rugose-punctate; elytra posteriorly somewhat widened, deeply punctate-striate, the punctures dark, the interstices punctulate, some strongly and densely (punctured), others paler reddish-ferruginous, finely punctulate; underside of body and legs pitchy.   Length 8 mm.  Parao River.

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 90).
