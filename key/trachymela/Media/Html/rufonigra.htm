rufonigra

Name

Paropsis rufonigra Chapuis, 1877

Corresponds to

No known correspondence with any of the species codes.

Diagnosis and Notes

Blackburn (1896, p.640) indicates that he has not seen the type of rufonigra and considers it too insufficiently described to include in his key.  The description is not enough to allow it to be associated with a single species.  It is most likely to be an orbicularis-group species such as Ta30 or Ta36, though several other species would also fit the description reasonably well.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 1) by ChatGPT, 04/2026:

Somewhat rounded, moderately convex, red-and-black. Head punctate, red, dark at the base and apex. Pronotum more sparsely and finely punctate, with very small punctures intermixed, somewhat depressed at the sides, there with larger punctures and a blunt tubercle.  Elytra moderately punctate-striate, with some of the interstices here and there raised, shining, toward the apex nodulose-costate, dark reddish, variegated with pitchy markings.  Length 5 mm.  Australia.

References

Chapuis, F. 1877, "Synopsis des espèces du genre Paropsis", Annales de la Société Entomologique de Belgique (Comptes-rendus), vol. 20, 67-101 (page 97).
