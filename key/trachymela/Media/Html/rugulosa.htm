Name

Paropsis rugulosa Boisduval, 1835

Diagnosis and Notes

Chapuis (1877) lists this species in his treatment of the species under his 'Group IV', which includes the species with about 20 elytral series and which includes the bulk of the species now placed in Trachymela.  Blackburn completely ignores this species in his own treatment of the genus.  Boisduval's description is very brief and could apply to many species of Trachymela.  No size is given for the species.  It is currently listed as a Paropsis, but should be changed to Trachymela rugulosa Boisduval (comb. nov), but as a nomen dubium.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 1) by ChatGPT, 04/2026:

Dark red, without any markings, with the legs somewhat paler; pronotum finely granulate; elytra very densely punctate-granulate.

Of a dark red colour, without any spots, with the legs a little lighter; the pronotum slightly roughened, and the elytra covered with impressed and roughened punctures.
New Holland. Collection of M. Dupont.
