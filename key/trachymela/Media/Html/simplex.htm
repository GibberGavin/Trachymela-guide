simplex

Name

Paropsis simplex Blackburn, 1897

Corresponds to

This is very likely Ta175, by comparison with the description, type locality and images and measurements of the type specimen (BMNH).  The type specimen is 5.05 mm in length.  Its aedeagus had been dissected out at some time and its shape and the long, thin and gently curved flagellum are effectively identical to those of Ta175.  The measured LPE of the type (1.54 mm) and the interocular distance (A-A) are in close accord with these values for similar-sized specimens of Ta175.

Diagnosis and Notes

In his revision of the genus Paropsis (Blackburn, 1896), he places simplex into his "Group iv", a mixed bag of species characterized by (1) the sides of the prothorax evenly arched (not mucronate in front), (2) the puncturation of the elytra in about 20 rows, and (3) the elytra devoid of verrucae.   This group includes just a few species of non-verrucose Trachymela - the bulk of its species are now placed in Peltoschema or Paropsisterna.  In his key to the species in this group, simplex is identified by:

A. Elytra with a distinct subbasal impression;

BB. Lateral margins of prothorax not or scarcely explanate;

CC. The subbasal impression of the elytra very feeble;

D. Size very small.  The scutellum punctured.

Note how Blackburn indicates that the subbasal impression is both distinct (A) and very feeble (CC)!

Type specimen

Location: BMNH

Label data: /“Type” (red-bordered circle)/ “6255 N.W.A. T.”/ “Blackburn coll. 1910-236.”/ “Paropsis simplex Blackb.”/.

Male; Size: 5.05 (L) x 3.95 (W) x 2.3 (H) mm; A-A: 0.91 mm.

A completely brown species lacking verrucae, no transverse ridge, punctures uniform, some interstices just slightly thickened longitudinally, especially near the apex.

Aedeagus, LPE: 1.54 mm.

Original description

Translation of original description (Figure 4) by ChatGPT, 04/2026:

Rather broadly ovate; shining; moderately convex; with the greatest height (seen from the side) situated about the middle of the elytral margin; entirely reddish-brown.  Head closely and somewhat strongly punctulate.  Prothorax broader than long in the ratio of about 2⅝:1; transversely impressed behind the apex; widened from the apex to well beyond the middle; rather closely and moderately strongly punctulate (at the sides coarsely rugulose); sides moderately rounded, not flattened; posterior angles obtuse.  Scutellum punctulate.  Elytra scarcely depressed beneath the humeral callus; behind the base lightly transversely impressed; rather closely and moderately strongly punctulate, somewhat arranged in rows; marginal area rather narrow (this posteriorly directed obliquely outward fairly strongly), moderately distinct from the disc; inner margin of the humeral callus slightly more distant from the suture than from the lateral margin of the elytra.  Basal ventral segment rather sparsely and fairly finely punctulate.  Length 2⅖ lines [~5 mm]; width 1 9/10 lines [~4 mm].

References
